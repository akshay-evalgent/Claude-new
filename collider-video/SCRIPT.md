## Fact notes

- SSC site: Ellis County, around Waxahachie, about 30 miles south of Dallas. The ring was 87 km (54 mi) around, 20 TeV per beam, 40 TeV collisions.
- Approved by President Reagan in January 1987 (his "Throw deep" line is widely reported). Texas site chosen in November 1988. Cancelled by Congress in October 1993.
- About 14 miles (roughly 22–23 km) of tunnel and 17 shafts had been dug, and about $2 billion spent.
- Cost estimates: about $4.4B (1987), about $8.25B (1991), more than $11B (1993).
- Magnet aperture widened from 4 cm to 5 cm in 1990.
- The space station survived the House 216–215 in June 1993.
- The LHC (27 km) announced the Higgs discovery on 4 July 2012. The Texas site was bought by a chemical company in 2012.
