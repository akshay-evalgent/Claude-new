## 2. Thumbnails: run all three in Test & Compare

On the upload page, under **Thumbnail**, choose **Test & compare** and upload A, B and C. YouTube shows
each one to a share of viewers and keeps the one with the highest watch-time share, usually within a few
days to two weeks. This gets the most out of each impression without guessing.

| | Idea | Why it can win |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Bright flat cross-section: farmland, a buried 54-mile ring, CERN's ring inside it for scale | Same formula as the reference: one scale comparison and two boxed labels. It stands out against dark feeds |
| **B** | Real abandoned building, ground cut away to a glowing tunnel, "$2 BILLION / ABANDONED" and a red arrow | Real photo, a big number and an arrow: the most "clickbait" of the three |
| **C** | The ring drawn to true scale over Waxahachie against CERN's LHC, "3× BIGGER" | A scale shock you can read in half a second. The ratio is accurate (87.1 km against 26.7 km) |

If you can only upload one, use **B**. It reads best at phone size and carries the strongest emotion.

All three are 1280x720 JPEGs under 2 MB, as YouTube requires.
