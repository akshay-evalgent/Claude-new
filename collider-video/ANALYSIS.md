# Style breakdown: "The Real Reason Why the Soviets Abandoned Earth's Deepest Hole"

Reference: Zoufry, 8 min 34 s, 1280x720 at 30 fps, uploaded 5 Nov 2026, about 3.06 M views.
Measured from the downloaded file: 72 detected hard cuts, median shot length 2.8 s. Camera
moves inside the 3D shots hide many more cuts, so the real pace is a new image every 3 to 5 s.
The narration is 1,261 words in 514 s, about 147 words a minute: calm, flat and never hyped.

## 1. The script formula

The video is one long promise followed by three chapters. Every beat below maps to a timestamp.

| Beat | Time | What it does | Kola line |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary object | 0:00-0:20 | A place you would drive past, given in precise geography | "a rusted metal cap... looks like a manhole cover" |
| What lies under it | 0:20-0:40 | One hard number for size, then a superlative | "23 cm wide... over 12 km... deepest hole ever" |
| "But it was abandoned" | 0:40 | The twist, said in four words | |
| Goal vs result | 0:45-1:05 | The target number against the number reached | "planning to hit 15,000, but they never got there" |
| Title + three questions | 1:05-1:20 | "This is the story of X. Why... What... And why..." | These questions become the three chapters |
| Chapter 1: why | 1:20-3:40 | Knowledge gap, a rival who failed, the Cold War motive, why it's hard, the custom machine, milestone years | Ends on a cliffhanger: "the planet had something else to say" |
| Chapter 2: what they found | 3:40-6:20 | A numbered list: "The first surprise... The second... Then... Deeper down... Then came..." | Each item gets one predicted number against one actual number |
| Chapter 3: why it ended | 6:20-8:00 | Mechanism, then "the official reason was money", then "But the engineers have said something else since" | Closes with a symmetry line: "When the Cold War ended, the hole ended with it." |
| Callback ending | 8:00-8:25 | Back to the object from 0:00. "Today, there's nothing to see." A final unanswerable question. "Nobody knows." | |

The writing rules that make it work:
- Short declarative sentences, often fragments: "No signs. No fences."
- At least one hard number in almost every sentence, nearly always with a year attached.
- Expected against actual is the engine: "predicted 100 °C, but it was 180."
- Mild mystery framing, then the real answer, and an explicit debunk of the myth ("not the fake audio hoax").
- No call to action and no "subscribe" in the narration. The ending just stops.

## 2. The visual system

| Element | How it looks | When it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Near-black `#121212` to `#181818`, a faint diamond cross-hatch, vignette | Every graphic scene |
| Accent | One amber-yellow, about `#F8B400`, and nothing else | Lines, numbers, keywords, tape labels |
| Labels | Inter-style bold grotesque, tight tracking: `KOLA`, `12KM`, `23 cm wide` | Places, measurements |
| Small caps | Tiny tracked uppercase: `NORTHWESTERN RUSSIA`, `GRANITE: PLASTIC STATE` | Sub-labels under a bold label |
| Stat callout | Small year above, huge yellow number, a thin rule, tiny caps caption | Milestones (1979 / 9.5 KM / WORLD RECORD) |
| Chapter card | Serif text typed on letter by letter with a cursor, on dark grunge, then dissolves | 3 times |
| Vintage map | Cream paper card on black, serif title, water black and land cream, thin graticule, region traced in yellow | Location reveals |
| Schematic | Thin grey strata lines, a yellow probe or line, leader lines that snap at 90° to callouts | Every technical explanation |
| Photo card | Tilted print with a yellow tape label (`Soviet research station`) | Introducing a real place |
| Lower-left caption | White bold year or name typed on (`1989`, `Mohole`, `Project shut down`) | Dating archive shots |
| Archive / 3D | Old film footage and clay-white 3D figures | About half the runtime |

Motion rules: nothing is ever still. Every shot has a slow push-in or drift. Lines draw on
rather than appear, and numbers and text type on. Transitions are mostly hard cuts, with
dissolves only into and out of chapter cards.

## 3. Sound

- An ambient cinematic bed sits under the whole video, swelling at the title and chapter cards
  (`[music]` in the captions marks the swells).
- The voice sits well on top of the music (about -18.7 dB mean overall, peaks at -0.2 dB).
- Typewriter ticks under typed text, low hits on reveals, risers into chapter cards.

## 4. How the remake copies it

- The same beat sheet, beat for beat (see `SCRIPT.md`): ordinary place, what's underneath, "but
  it was abandoned", goal against reached, three questions, three chapters, a numbered list of
  problems, the official reason against the real reason, the symmetry line, callback, "Nobody knows."
- The same palette, type, stat callouts, maps, schematics, chapter cards, lower-left captions,
  grain and constant drift.
- What is not copied: archive footage and 3D figures. Wikimedia was unreachable from the build
  machine and no footage was licensed, so those beats are motion graphics instead (rings,
  magnets, cost bars, a vote counter, tunnel progress).
- The reference's "made by humans" opening card is left out, because this remake is generated.
