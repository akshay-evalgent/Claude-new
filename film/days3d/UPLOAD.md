## 1. The long film

**Title (lead):** `30,000 DAYS | An Animated Short Film`

This is the title format of the channel we studied (Day One Films: `DISCIPLINE | An Animated Short Film`, 5.84M views). We copy the format, not the words: the study found 38 channels that re-used the word DISCIPLINE got under 1% of its views between them.

**Title to test against it:** `What If You Could See How Many Days You Have Left?`
(Use Test & Compare for titles if your channel has it; otherwise run the lead for 7 days, then switch if click-through is below your channel average.)

**Thumbnails:** upload all three to Test & Compare. A (`DAYS LEFT.`) is the lead. All three follow Day One Films' thumbnail format: a near-black frame, one warm light source, one character with an exaggerated face, one prop that carries the idea, and one or two words in heavy white rounded capitals ending in a full stop. The art and the character are ours.

## 3. How the math is used

From `film/viral-study`: a film spreads only if each view earns more than one further view (R > 1). R is a product of click-through, how long people watch, whether they are satisfied, and whether they share. Above 1, the film reaches a share z of everyone who cares about the theme (z = 1 − e^(−R·z)), so the size of that audience sets the ceiling. From R 0.90 to 1.10 the same 10K seed goes from 98K to 3.6M views.

| Lever | What we did | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Audience pool | Time is one of the biggest open themes in the scan: the top 40 results have a median of 9.5M views (32 of 40 over 1M) and 15 to 17% are copies, against 80 to 95% for self-improvement themes. No dialogue; captions in 7 languages; 3 translated titles. | Traffic by geography after 7 days |
| Click-through | The winning channel's thumbnail and title format, with our own art. Three thumbnails in Test & Compare. A number in thumbnail A. | CTR against your channel's 28-day average |
| Hook | The notification arrives with a buzz at 0.45 s and shows the number at 0.6 s. Frame one is lit. | Retention at 0:10 |
| Pace | A new picture, light or number at least every 2 s (see pre-flight); characters animate on twos, cameras move every frame | Flat or rising retention through 0:26 to 1:00 |
| The rewatched beat | The daydream lands at 1:00 (36%), where the most-replayed moment of *I'll Start Tomorrow…* sits (replay 1.00) | A bump in "most replayed" at 1:00 |
| Payoff | The two score tags on the roof at 1:38 to 1:42 (60%): 24,880 stars with 3 days left, against 1,204 stars with 18,250 left | Retention holding through 1:30 to 1:50 |
| Emotion | Dread turns to awe and lift; nobody dies on screen (sadness lowers sharing; awe and elevation raise it) | Likes per view |
| Share trigger | The last line asks for one act today. The pinned comment asks for one item from your LATER box, which is easy to answer and to send to a friend. | Shares per 1,000 views; comments that name a thing |
| Shots on goal | The film and the Short, plus the LANTERN film and Shorts. A log-normal fit gave about 7% per film of passing 1M, so volume matters: 10 films give about a 51% chance of at least one | Run `film/viral-study/theme_radar.py` before choosing film three |

**The R read, 24 to 48 hours after posting.** Export daily (or hourly) views from Studio and run:

```
python3 film/lantern/r_read.py --csv export.csv
```
It tells you whether views are still growing period over period (R > 1) or fading, and if fading, roughly where the total will land. If R is under 1 by day 3, change the thumbnail first; it is the cheapest lever.

## 4. Pre-flight results

| Check | Rule | Film | Short |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frame one brightness (luma) | > 20 | 35.4 | 38.3 |
| First motion | ≤ 1 s | 0.0 s | 0.0 s |
| Longest unchanged stretch | ≤ 2.0 s | 1.6 s | 1.6 s |
| Longest black | ≤ 0.3 s | 0.1 s | 0.0 s |
| Frozen end hold | < 1.4 s | 0.9 s | 0.0 s |
| Loudness | −14 LUFS ± 1 | -13.9 LUFS | -13.8 LUFS |
| True peak | ≤ −1 dBTP | -3.6 dBTP | -3.9 dBTP |

Every rule passes on both files. Numbers are measured from the finished MP4s (`release/preflight.json`).

Before posting, watch it once with sound on headphones. The mix was balanced by measurement, not by ear.

## 6. Rebuild

```
cd film/days3d
npm install
node render.js --jobs 2 --out out/shots s01_lock s02_card1 …            # 21 shots, 1920×1080, 24 fps
node render.js --jobs 2 --w 1080 --h 1920 --out out/shots_v s01_lock:0-5 … # the Short's 8 ranges (see score.py SHORT)
python3 assemble.py master && python3 assemble.py short && python3 assemble.py preflight
python3 captions.py out/final/captions
node shoot.js thumb_a:1920x1080 thumb_b:1920x1080 thumb_c:1920x1080
```
