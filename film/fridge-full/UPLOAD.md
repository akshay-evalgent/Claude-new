**Description:**

Fifty thousand years ago, a hunter brings down an antelope. More meat than his family can eat, and in this heat it will rot within days. No fridge. No ice. No salt. So how did ancient humans keep food from spoiling?

From 400,000-year-old deer bones used like cans, to a 9,200-year-old fish pit in Sweden, to grain stores built before farming, this is the story of how humans beat an enemy they couldn't even see. And why the smartest fridge of all was other people.
