## 3. How the math is used

From `film/viral-study`: a film spreads only if each view earns more than one further view (R > 1). R is a product of click-through, how long people watch, whether they are satisfied, and whether they share. Above 1, the film reaches a share z of everyone who cares about the theme (z = 1 − e^(−R·z)), so the size of that audience sets the ceiling. From R 0.90 to 1.10 the same 10K seed goes from 98K to 3.6M views.

| Lever | What we did | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Audience pool | "Try again" is one of the most open themes in the scan: the top 40 results have a median of 3.4M views (27 of 40 over 1M) and only 7% are copies, against 80 to 95% for discipline-style titles. No dialogue; captions in 7 languages; 3 translated titles. | Traffic by geography after 7 days |
| Click-through | The winning channel's title and thumbnail format, with our own art. Three thumbnails in Test & Compare. A number in the title and in thumbnail A. | CTR against your channel's 28-day average |
| Hook | Frame one is lit and moving: the bare canvas being laid by the knife, then the first plane painted into being. | Retention at 0:10 |
| Pace | One action, one sound, one cut: each montage attempt is one bar of the 96 BPM grid, and each lands a note one step higher, so the sequence climbs | Flat or rising retention through 0:20 to 0:45 |
| The low point | Attempt 213 at 0:55 (37%) and a hard silence at 1:05 (43%), where DISCIPLINE puts its "Rock bottom" chapter (0:50 to 1:00 of 2:38, 32 to 38%) | A dip that recovers by 1:15 |
| Payoff | Plane 214 is thrown at 1:36 (64%); the colour comes back at 2:03 (82%). The motif that never resolved while she failed resolves there | Retention holding through 1:30 to 2:10 |
| Emotion | Frustration turns to lift and awe; the colour re-laid by the knife is the visual payoff (awe and elevation raise sharing) | Likes per view |
| Share trigger | A screenshot line ("Most people stop at 213."), like DISCIPLINE's "Day one, or one day."; the pinned comment asks for a number, which is easy to answer | Shares per 1,000 views; comments with numbers |
| Shots on goal | Film three, after LANTERN and 30,000 DAYS. A log-normal fit gave about 7% per film of passing 1M, so volume matters: 10 films give about a 51% chance of at least one | Run `film/viral-study/theme_radar.py` before choosing film four |

**The R read, 24 to 48 hours after posting.** Export daily (or hourly) views from Studio and run:

```
python3 film/lantern/r_read.py --csv export.csv
```
It tells you whether views are still growing period over period (R > 1) or fading, and if fading, roughly where the total will land. If R is under 1 by day 3, change the thumbnail first; it is the cheapest lever.

## 6. Rebuild

```
cd film/attempt214
node render.js --jobs 3 --out out/shots s01_canvas s02_tilt s03_first s04_montage s05_room s06_213 s07_silence s08_sign s09_climb s10_throw s11_flight s12_catch s13_colour s14_pattern s15_end
node render.js --jobs 3 --w 1080 --h 1920 --out out/shots_v s04_montage:0-10 s06_213:0.6-6.6 s08_sign:1.2-7.2 s09_climb:4-10 s10_throw:2.5-7.5 s11_flight:0-10 s13_colour:1.5-9.5 s15_end:0-4.6
python3 assemble.py master && python3 assemble.py short && python3 assemble.py preflight
python3 captions.py out/final/captions
node render.js --still thumb_a@0 thumb_b@0 thumb_c@0
```
