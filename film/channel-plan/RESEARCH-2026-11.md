# Topic research: what's getting millions of views now (14 November 2026)

**Method.** I pulled YouTube search results directly, sorted by view count, for 34 searches in our niche, both all-time and this year only. I also pulled the full upload lists of the channels behind the biggest hits: Ink Explainer, Zenn, Axen, Lore Explained, Relic Explains, Super Joy Animations, GranKhelafa, PROF. STICKMAN and Paint The Facts. Views are as of today.

## The big picture

- **"What Did Ancient Humans…?" animated explainers are the hottest format on YouTube right now.** Small channels are getting millions of views:

  | Video | Channel | Views | Age |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | What Did Ancient Humans Actually Do All Day? | Ink Explainer (170K subscribers) | 18.8M | 2 months |
  | What Did Ancient Humans Do at Night? | Zenn | 8.2M | 7 months |
  | Why Did Humans Start Wearing Clothes? | Relic Explains (21.6K subscribers) | 5.9M | 1 month |
  | What Did Ancient Humans Do For Pleasure? | Lore Explained (12.1K subscribers) | 5.6M | 3 weeks |
  | What Did Ancient Humans Do All Day Before Jobs Existed? | Axen | 3.4M | 6 months |

- **It's a hit-driven format.** Each of these channels' other uploads mostly get 1K–50K views. A topic plus its packaging produces one outlier, and the outlier carries the channel.
- **Copies of a hot topic still win.** "Why did humans start wearing clothes?" has been made by eight channels in the last six months, and every version has 338K–5.9M views. One is two weeks old and already at 669K.
- **Most hits are 7–12 minutes long.** Ours run about 15 minutes.
