# "Could a harpoon really pin Dom's car?": an original physics Short

**Why it's an original and not the clip:** the requested source,
[youtube.com/watch?v=cOdynPv8cok](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cOdynPv8cok), is Universal's
official *Fate of the Furious* clip on "The Fast Saga" channel. Ripping it and adding commercial
songs would be claimed by Content ID (or blocked or struck), and since Oct 2026 YouTube gives less
Shorts reach to channels that mostly re-upload others' videos. In the playbook that is the
"stop subtracting" step, so we made an original explainer about the scene instead. It contains
no film footage, stills, logos or posters, and every graphic and sound is generated by
`make.py`.

Output: `film/harpoon/out/harpoon-physics.mp4`, 1080x1920, 30 fps, 34.2 s (not in git).
Rebuild with `python3 film/harpoon/make.py`. `look/sheet.jpg` is a contact sheet every 1.5 s.

## The physics on screen (back-of-envelope, stated as ≈)

| claim | assumption | value |
|---|---|---|
| his tyres can only push ≈ 8 kN | 1,700 kg car, half the weight on the rear axle, tyre grip μ ≈ 1.0; horsepower can't exceed grip | 0.5 × 1700 × 9.81 × 1.0 = 8.3 kN |
| one 10 mm steel cable holds ≈ 60 kN | minimum breaking load of 10 mm 6×19 steel wire rope | about 60-64 kN |
| an anchor car with locked brakes holds ≈ 17 kN | 2.3 t car sliding on locked wheels, μ ≈ 0.75 | 0.75 × 2300 × 9.81 = 16.9 kN |
| a hook in 0.8 mm body steel tears out at ≈ 5 kN | tear-out 2 × edge distance × thickness × 0.6 × tensile strength; 15 mm edge, 0.8 mm mild steel at 340 MPa | 4.9 kN |
| stopping him from 100 km/h in 20 m takes ≈ 33 kN | m v² / 2s with 1,700 kg, 27.8 m/s, 20 m (about 2 g) | 32.8 kN |

**Verdict:** holding a car that is trying to drive away is plausible if the hooks bite the frame
(cable 60 kN and anchor car 17 kN both beat the tyres' 8 kN). Through the body panels it isn't,
because a hook tears out at about 5 kN. At speed, the bodywork rips long before 33 kN.
