## Description

```
What if Santa flew close to the speed of light?

Einstein showed that the faster you move, the slower your clock ticks. Push Santa fast enough and
six hours on his watch become 23 years at the North Pole: every second for him is about nine hours at home.

The numbers:
• Every child under 15 on Earth is about 2 billion children in about 1 billion homes (World Bank).
• The shortest route through those homes is about 94 million km.
• Midnight to six in every time zone gives him a 33-hour window, so he must average about 795 km a second.
• For 6 hours to equal 23 years, his clock must run about 33,600 times slow. That needs about
  99.99999996% of the speed of light.

At the real delivery speed, relativity barely touches him: his watch would end the night about half a
second slow. The 23 years is the "what if".

Made with AI-generated video (Google Flow, Veo), AI narration and an original score.

#Shorts #Physics #Santa #Christmas #Einstein #Relativity #TimeDilation #Science #WhatIf
```

## When to post

- **Season:** Shorts about Christmas climb from late November. Post by 1 December, and re-share it to
  community posts on 20 to 24 December.
- **Time of day:** early evening in your main audience's time zone, when Shorts traffic peaks.
- **First hour:** reply to the first comments and pin the physics comment straight away.
