Fifty thousand years ago, a band of about thirty people sets up camp for the night. No president. No police. No written laws. No prison. So who was in charge, who settled fights, and what stopped the strongest man from taking whatever he wanted?

From a chimp who became alpha by banging kerosene cans, to Kalahari hunters who insulted the best hunter's meat to "cool his heart", Inuit song duels, police who existed only during the bison hunt, the first kings and the oldest written words for freedom, this is the story of how humans lived for hundreds of thousands of years without government, but never without rules.
