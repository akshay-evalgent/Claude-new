Picture your evening: the couch, the blanket, the phone. Now take the phone away. No TV, no internet, for 300,000 years. Were ancient humans bored out of their minds?

Not even close. Rats laugh when you tickle them, apes giggle, and young chimps play with stick "dolls". Hunter-gatherers had more free time than farmers, spent their nights telling stories, gambled with bark discs, and played with knucklebones. People were playing the Royal Game of Ur 4,500 years ago (and you can still play it today), juggling 4,000 years ago, and telling a fart joke that is nearly 4,000 years old. This is the story of how humans had fun for 300,000 years without a single screen.
