Fifty thousand years ago, a hunter brings down an antelope. More meat than his family can eat, and under this sun it will start to rot within days. No fridge. No ice. No salt. So how did ancient humans keep food from spoiling?

From deer bones used like cans 400,000 years ago, to a 9,200-year-old fish pit in Sweden, to grain stores built before farming, this is the story of how humans fought an enemy they couldn't even see. And why the smartest fridge of all was other people.
