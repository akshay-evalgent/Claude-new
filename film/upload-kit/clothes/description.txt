You are the only animal on Earth that gets dressed. So when did humans start wearing clothes, and why? Clothes rot, so there's no ancient wardrobe to dig up. Instead, scientists followed three clues: your skin, a tiny bug, and a needle.

You have about as many hair follicles as a chimp. Lice DNA suggests clothing is at least 83,000 years old, maybe 170,000. Your toe bones remember the first shoes. Plus 30,000-year-old dyed fibres (including pink), 13,000 ivory beads sewn onto clothes, Ötzi's five-animal outfit, the world's oldest woven dress found in a bag of old linen, and why trousers may have been invented for riding horses.
