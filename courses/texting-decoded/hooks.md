# Texting, decoded — hooks (gate 3)

Five per episode. Each hook is frame 1 (what is on screen before a word is spoken, with its
3–6 words of text) and the first sentence. Chosen hooks are marked once you pick.

## 1/7 — 'ok' vs 'ok.'

| | Frame 1 | First sentence |
|---|---|---|
| 1A | A phone lights up on a dark table. One bubble: "ok." Text: ok vs ok. | "'ok.' Full stop ke saath. Tum jaante ho." |
| 1B | A thumb types "ok", stops, then adds the full stop. Text: watch the pause | "Usne 'ok' likha. Ruka. Phir full stop lagaya." |
| 1C | Two bubbles side by side, A: "ok" B: "ok." Text: which one is angry? | "Inme se kaunsa gussa hai?" |
| 1D | A chat: "ok." then three hours of empty timestamps scrolling. Text: 1 full stop, 3 hours | "Ek full stop. Teen ghante ki chuppi." |
| 1E | An older person's chat, every message ends with a full stop. Text: papa's ok. ≠ her ok. | "Papa ka 'ok.' aur uska 'ok.' ek nahi hai." |
