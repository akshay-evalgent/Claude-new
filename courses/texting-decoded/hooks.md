# Texting, decoded — hooks (gate 3)

English, for an international audience. Five per episode: frame 1 (what is on screen before a
word is spoken, with its 3–6 words of text) and the first sentence. ★ = recommended.

| | Frame 1 | First sentence |
|---|---|---|
| 1A ★ | A phone lights up on a dark table. One grey bubble: "ok." Text: ok vs ok. | "'ok.' With the period. You know." |
| 1B | A thumb types "ok", stops, then adds the period. Text: watch the pause | "They typed 'ok'. Paused. Then added the period." |
| 1C | Two bubbles side by side, A: "ok" B: "ok." Text: which one is mad? | "Which one of these is mad at you?" |
