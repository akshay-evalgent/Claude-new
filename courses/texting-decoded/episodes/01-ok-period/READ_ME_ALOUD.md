# 1/7 'ok' vs 'ok.' — read this aloud

One WhatsApp voice note, in your own voice. It gets converted to Theo afterwards; your pauses,
breaths and timing are kept exactly, so the comedy is yours. A phone at a hand's width in a
quiet room is enough. Speak low and dry, like telling a friend something obvious; never
"announcer". Where it says **(pause)**, actually stop for about a second. Leave about two
seconds of silence between numbered lines. If a line goes wrong, just say it again after a
pause; the best read of each line is picked automatically.

1. 'ok.' With the period. **(pause)** Congratulations. **(pause)** You're in trouble.
   *flat; "congratulations" with zero warmth; a tiny shrug on "you're in trouble"*

2. They typed 'ok'. **(beat)** Paused. **(pause)** Went back for the period. **(pause)** Nobody does that by accident.
   *slow and deliberate, as if describing a crime scene*

3. Same question. **(beat)** Three answers. **(pause)** One is a threat.
   *"threat" said lightly, almost bored*

4. 'okay': happy. **(beat)** 'ok': busy. **(beat)** 'ok.': you forgot something. **(pause)** Good luck.
   *read the three like a list; slow down on the third; "good luck" sympathetic and flat*

5. Except. **(pause)** One kind of person breaks this rule.
   *quieter, like a secret; the pause after "except" is the joke setup*

6. Dads. **(beat)** Bosses. **(beat)** People who sign their texts. **(pause)** Their 'ok.' is just 'ok'. **(pause)** Pattern first.
   *fond on "dads"; a smile you can hear on "sign their texts"; firm on "pattern first"*

7. Now you. **(beat)** A… **(beat)** or B? **(long pause)** Careful. **(pause)** One of them is your dad.
   *inviting on "now you"; then completely straight-faced*

8. A. **(pause)** First period she's ever used. **(pause)** Dad's just Dad.
   *quiet and certain; the last three words with a shrug*

9. Bonus. **(beat)** A lone 'k' is a door closing. **(pause)** On your face. **(long pause)** Two of seven: left on read.
   *lighter; "on your face" said kindly; the last line slow and clear, ending on a small lift*

Say "'ok.'" as "okay, period" only if it feels natural; otherwise just "okay" with a little
stop after it. Say "'k'" as the letter K.
