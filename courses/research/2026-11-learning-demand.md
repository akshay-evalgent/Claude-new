rising.json (rising related queries, 12 months, US and worldwide), supply_us.json (YouTube US results).

## Reading
Growing demand with thin Shorts supply: AI coding/assistant tools (Claude Code, Codex, NotebookLM)
and American mahjong. Both are rule- or interface-based, so they can be taught fully in motion graphics.
ASL, tai chi and crochet are growing too, but need real hands and bodies and are already well served in Shorts.

Caveats: Trends numbers are relative, not absolute; rising percentages can come from small bases;
one 12-month window. Market context: microlearning market forecasts of 11.8-14.4% CAGR to 2031-32
(Mordor Intelligence, GII Research, Reanin) — vendor summaries, not independently verified.
