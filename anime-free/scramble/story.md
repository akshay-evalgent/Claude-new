# Scramble

A 20-second shonen fight, original characters, on a busy Tokyo street at night.

A rainy night at a huge neon-lit scramble crossing in Tokyo, packed with people under umbrellas. Akane, 19, a street
kickboxer in a red bomber jacket, has finally tracked down Jin, 22, a calm silver-haired fighter in a white hoodie. She
calls him out in the middle of the crowd. He asks if she really means to do it here. She answers by sprinting at him.
They trade fast punches and blocks on the wet asphalt while the crowd backs away into a ring. Her spinning kick lands on
his raised forearm and sends him sliding back through the rainwater. He lowers his guard and admits it was not bad. She
grins and raises her fists again: she is not done yet.

Grounded hand-to-hand contact, no superpowers, no blood. Quiet standoff, then one fast burst, then a cocky ending.
Japanese voices with English subtitles.
