Location painting for an anime film. {{STYLE}}

The location: a huge scramble crossing in central Tokyo on a rainy night, seen from eye level at the edge of the crossing looking across it. Wide white zebra stripes on black wet asphalt that mirrors the lights, tall buildings on every side covered in glowing neon signs and giant video screens that show only abstract colour shapes, overhead power lines, traffic lights, a dense crowd of pedestrians under transparent and dark umbrellas crossing in every direction, small and seen from behind or far away, with no close faces. Fine rain, light haze, puddles. Wide 16:9 establishing view, deep perspective down the avenue. No readable text, no lettering, no logos, no vehicles in the crossing.
