# Prompt — Canon DSLR camera in interactive 3D

Copy everything below the line.

---

Build me a Canon DSLR camera in interactive 3D, in one HTML file with Three.js.

Make it look real: the black magnesium body with the rubber grip, the lens on the front with glass you can look into, the mode dial, the shutter button and the hot shoe on top, the screen on the back, and inside it the parts you never get to see. The lens elements lined up in the barrel, the aperture blades that iris open and shut, the reflex mirror sitting at 45 degrees, the pentaprism above it feeding the viewfinder, the two shutter curtains, the image sensor behind them, the main board, the battery and the memory card, and the red ring around the lens. Press the shutter and it happens in order: the mirror flips up, the first curtain opens, the sensor catches the light, the second curtain closes, the mirror drops back, and I hear the clack. Turn the aperture and the blades close down and open up. Turn the focus ring and the inner elements slide. I can drag to orbit around it and scroll to zoom.

Add an Explode button. When I press it, every part slides apart in layers along the lens axis with its name on a label. Press it again and it all snaps back together. When I hover a part, it lights up and shows one line on what it does.

No images. Do not ask me any questions. Just build it.
