# Video mood board — Canon DSLR, inside and out

A 30-second vertical short (1080x1920, 24 fps) made from the interactive 3D build. Same shape as the watch film: one object, one light, one sound, one reveal.

## One line

A camera you think you know, taken apart in the dark until you can see how a photograph actually happens.

## Mood words

Precise. Quiet. Engineered. Black on black with one red line. The feeling of holding a tool that is heavier than it looks.

## Palette

| Role | Colour | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | near-black, slightly cold `#0B0C0E` | background, body shadow side |
| Body | matte charcoal `#1C1D20` | magnesium shell, grip, dials |
| Glass | deep blue-green `#1E3A3C` with a white rim light | front element, pentaprism, sensor filter |
| Metal | cool silver `#B8BCC2` | lens mount, hot shoe, screws, mirror hinge |
| Accent | red `#C8102E`, used once per frame at most | lens ring, the AF-confirm dot, the one label that matters |
| Light trace | pale warm `#F2E9D8` | the shaft of light that passes through the lens to the sensor |

One accent. Nothing else is red.

## Light

- One large soft key from top-left, like a studio softbox. It reads the brushed metal and the rubber texture differently and that is the whole point.
- A thin cold rim from the back-right so the body separates from the black ground.
- No fill. Let the shadow side go to black.
- The lens glass carries a single long reflection, a window shape, that slides as the camera orbits.
- When the shutter fires, a short pale beam crosses from the front element to the sensor for 4 frames, then is gone.

## Materials, close up

- Rubber grip: fine diamond texture, zero gloss.
- Magnesium body: matte with a faint sheen only on edges.
- Lens barrel: satin black with engraved white distance scale.
- Glass: deep, dark, reflective; you can look into it and see the aperture blades.
- Mirror: a true mirror; you see the lens reflected in it when it is down.
- Sensor: iridescent, green-magenta shift when the angle changes.
- Circuit board: dark green, gold traces, one tiny chip with a reflection.

## The camera filming the camera

- 85 mm feel, shallow depth of field, slow orbits only.
- Every move is a drift, never a whip. Nothing is handheld.
- Macro for the first 10 seconds. You do not see the whole camera until the explode.
- One rack-focus per shot at most, from a near part to a far part.

## Shot list (30 s)

| Time | Shot | Motion | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0-2.5 | Black. Then the red ring, edge-on, nothing else lit. | Slow push in. | Room tone. |
| 2.5-5.5 | Macro into the front element. Aperture blades visible deep inside. | Blades close from f/1.4 to f/8 and open again. | Faint mechanical rasp as the blades move. |
| 5.5-8.0 | Focus ring, engraved scale. | Ring turns 30 degrees; inner elements slide behind the glass. | Focus-ring friction, low. |
| 8.0-10.5 | Top plate. Mode dial, shutter button. | A finger-less press: the button goes down 1 mm. | Half-press click. |
| 10.5-13.0 | Side cutaway. Mirror at 45 degrees, pentaprism above. | Mirror flips up. | The first half of the shutter sound. |
| 13.0-15.0 | Behind the mirror: shutter curtains and the sensor. | First curtain opens, light beam hits the sensor, second curtain closes. | The second half of the shutter sound. This is the loudest moment in the film. |
| 15.0-17.0 | Mirror drops back. Hold. | Nothing moves for 1 s. | Silence, then room tone returns. |
| 17.0-24.0 | Explode. The whole camera, seen for the first time, parts sliding apart along the lens axis in layers. | One continuous slow orbit while parts separate. Labels fade in with each part, white, small. | A low sustained tone that rises as parts separate. |
| 24.0-27.0 | Exploded, fully open. | Orbit continues. | Tone holds. |
| 27.0-29.0 | Snap back. Parts return in reverse order. | Fast, 0.6 s, one movement. | A single soft clack as it closes. |
| 29.0-30.0 | The red ring again, same angle as the open. | Hold. | Room tone. End. |

Final shot share: 1 s of 30 = 3 %. End hold under 1.4 s.

## Motion rules

- Parts move on physical timing: mirror up in 4 frames, curtains in 2 frames each, mirror down in 6 frames.
- The explode takes 7 s. The snap back takes 0.6 s. The asymmetry is the idea.
- Labels never animate on their own. They appear with their part and go with their part.
- Orbit speed never changes except for the snap back.
- No dissolves between shots. Cuts land on the sounds in the table.

## Sound

- Real shutter recording, not a stock click. Two parts: mirror slap, then curtain travel. 1 s apart at 10.5-15.0 s so the viewer hears the sequence that the picture shows.
- One tone bed under the explode, starts at 17.0, ends on the snap.
- Mix to -14 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP. Silence at 15.0-17.0 is real silence, not a bed.

## Type and labels

- Labels: small, white, a monospace or a tight grotesk, all caps, 0.035 H minimum x-height so they pass the readability gate.
- One label per part. Name only. The one-line explanation lives in the interactive build, not in the film.
- No corner lanyard, no pinned caption. Nothing sits in the same place across shots.
- Title, if any, is the last frame: the camera name, small, centred under the red ring.

## Don'ts

- No white studio background. No gradient background. Black only.
- No lens flare except the single shutter beam.
- No second colour accent. No blue UI glow.
- Never show the full camera before the explode.
- No pinned "33 YEARS" style chrome; the watch film note's G5 lead applies here too.
