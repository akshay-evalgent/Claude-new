# Cast bible

The recurring characters for the prank-text Shorts. An episode picks one with `"contact": "<id>"`, and the renderer fills in the name and photo from `cast/cast.json`.

**The rule from Halo's data:** relationship drama flops; *something getting absurdly out of control* works with anyone. Each character gets that escalation in their own world: Mom's kitchen, Gary's office, Agent Miller's surveillance van.

**Rules for every character:**
- Made-up people only. Photos come from Flow and are never real or famous people.
- No jokes about anyone's ethnicity, religion or body type.
- Each character has their own texting voice and their own way of losing it.

## Dad (Driveway Police)🚨  (`dad`)
**Greg, Dad.**

- **How they text:** lowercase, no punctuation, short. Petty about the house, lawn and money.
- **How they lose it:** ALL CAPS comparisons: I ASKED FOR A ___ NOT A ___
- **Signature lines:** "i know what i saw" · "who is paying for this" · "ITS A ___ NOT A ___"
- **Premises:**
  - house and yard jobs that grow (snow, dog walker, hot tub, hedge maze, painter, roof)

**Photo prompt (Flow, portrait, crop to square):**
```
Casual smartphone photo of a made-up person (not a real or famous person), American dad in his mid 50s, short gray hair, gray mustache, red flannel shirt, holding a coffee mug in his driveway, slightly grumpy. Natural light, realistic, slightly imperfect framing.
```

## Mom 🌷 (read receipts ON)  (`mom`)
**Linda, Mom.**

- **How they text:** Perfect punctuation and capitals. Signs off "- Mom". Uses 👍 and 🙂 passive-aggressively. Replies "K." when upset.
- **How they lose it:** One huge caps line after calm ones: WHO RAISED YOU. Then she answers herself: I did. I'm calling your father.
- **Signature lines:** "Please call me." · "K." · "I'm not mad. I'm disappointed. 🙂"
- **Premises:**
  - the kitchen backsplash guy (backsplash → whole kitchen → indoor waterfall)
  - her garden gnome order (1 gnome → 40 → a gnome village with a mayor)
  - the house cleaner ("deep clean" → everything pressure-washed, including the couch)

**Photo prompt (Flow, portrait, crop to square):**
```
Casual smartphone photo of a made-up person (not a real or famous person), friendly American mom in her early 50s, shoulder-length auburn hair, cardigan, standing in a bright kitchen holding a mug, warm smile. Natural light, realistic, slightly imperfect framing.
```

**Agent Miller, "My FBI agent" (the webcam meme).**

## Using a character
- Put the photo in `chatvid/cast/photos/<id>.jpg`, square, at least 400 px. The renderer copies it into place. The full uncropped images are kept in `cast/photos/full/`; `dad-alt.jpg` is a second Dad we are not using, so the face stays the same across the series.
- In the episode, set `"contact": "mom"`. You can still give a full object to make a one-off character.
- **Crossovers** (Steve or the permit office reacting to Dad's projects) are a cheap way to make the series feel like one world.
