## Description

```
In 1994, Italian police officer Mauro Prosperi was running the Marathon des Sables, a six-day race across the Moroccan Sahara. On day four a sandstorm hit, and when it cleared he was alone and far off course.

He says he sheltered in an abandoned shrine and survived by drinking the blood of the bats living in its roof. On the ninth day a Tuareg girl herding goats found him. He had crossed into Algeria, almost 300 km off course, and had lost around 15 to 16 kg.

Four years later he went back and ran the race again.

An animated retelling of a true story, based on Prosperi's own account. The visuals are AI-generated illustrations, not real footage, and the maps are schematic.

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauro_Prosperi
```

## Settings

- **Thumbnail:** upload `cover.jpg` if Studio offers a custom thumbnail for the Short (this is rolling out to channels in the
  Partner Program). If it doesn't, choose the frame at about **0:04–0:05**, where he reaches up to the bat.
- **Audience:** *No, it's not made for kids.* The cartoon-like look can lead YouTube to classify it as made for kids,
  and that turns off comments and notifications.
- **Altered or synthetic content:** choose *Yes*. It shows a real person and real events in AI-generated
  pictures. For animated content the label only appears in the expanded description.
- **Subtitles:** Subtitles → Add language → English → Upload file → *With timing* → `captions.en.srt`.
  Set the video language to English.
- **Category:** Film & Animation (Education also fits).
- **Comments:** on. **Remixing:** allowed. **Paid promotion:** no.
