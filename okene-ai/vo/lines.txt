This man was trapped at the bottom of the ocean for almost three days. And he survived.
In 2013, Harrison Okene was the cook on a tugboat, off the coast of Nigeria.
Before dawn, in rough seas, the boat flipped over. It sank upside down, thirty meters to the seabed.
Harrison was in the toilet, in his underwear, as the water rushed in.
In total darkness, he felt his way through the flooded ship, and found a pocket of air.
All he had was one bottle of Coke, and two flashlights. Both went dark within a day.
He could hear fish outside, feeding on his crewmates.
He prayed, as every breath made the air more poisonous.
Sixty hours later, divers came to recover the bodies.
One saw a hand, and reached for it. The hand grabbed him back.
Of the twelve men on board, he was the only survivor.
He swore he'd never go back to the sea. Then he became a diver.
