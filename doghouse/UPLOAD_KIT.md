# YouTube upload kit: How a Doghouse Becomes a Secret Bunker

The format follows the reference short ("How a Rug Becomes a Secret Hideout") and its channel's
build stories, with the script written as a chain, as asked: it starts with something, that is not
enough, so it does something else, and something else, until it lands somewhere unexpected.

- The premise ("This doghouse has never had a dog. Because it isn't a doghouse.")
- Three "But..." turns, each answered with "So...": the walls cave in, so concrete rings; a hole isn't
  a hideout, so a room; every bunker needs a second way out, so a tunnel.
- The unexpected landing: the tunnel breaks into the neighbour's bunker, and his doghouse has no dog
  either.

Captions are burned in one word at a time, mid-frame. The reference channel has no doghouse video
(checked through its own search for "dog", "doghouse", "tunnel", "neighbor" and "bunker").

## The numbers in the script

A concept build, not a true story. The one number on screen, the 6 m shaft, is twelve 0.5 m precast
concrete rings, a standard way to line a dug shaft so the walls cannot cave in. The characters are
generic 3D people, not likenesses, and no brands or logos appear.
