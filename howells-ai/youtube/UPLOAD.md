## Description

```
In 2009, James Howells, a computer engineer in Newport, Wales, mined 8,000 bitcoin on his laptop, back when they were worth almost nothing. He kept the keys on an old hard drive in a drawer.

In 2013, while he was clearing out the house, the drive went into a bin bag by mistake, and the bag went to the town landfill. Months later, Bitcoin exploded.

For twelve years he tried to get it back. He asked the council to let him dig, offered them a share of the fortune, and put together investors, AI scanning and robot dogs. The council said no every time, citing its environmental permit. He sued for £495 million; in January 2025 the High Court dismissed the claim, and the appeal failed too. He offered to buy part of the landfill, which the council plans to close. In 2025 he said he had given up.

At bitcoin's peak in October 2025, 8,000 BTC was worth about $1 billion. Today it is worth roughly $480 million. The drive is still down there.

An animated retelling of a true story, based on news reports and the court ruling. The visuals are AI-generated illustrations, not real footage, and the price chart is approximate. Not financial advice.
```

## Settings

- **Thumbnail:** upload `cover.jpg` if Studio offers a custom thumbnail for the Short. If it doesn't, choose the frame at
  about **0:04**, the "$1,000,000,000" over the hard drive in the rubbish.
- **Audience:** *No, it's not made for kids.* The cartoon-like look can lead YouTube to classify it as made for kids,
  and that turns off comments, which this video is built to get.
- **Altered or synthetic content:** choose *Yes*. It shows a real person and real events in AI-generated
  pictures. For animated content the label only appears in the expanded description.
- **Subtitles:** Subtitles → Add language → English → Upload file → *With timing* → `captions.en.srt`.
  Set the video language to English.
- **Category:** Education (Film & Animation also fits).
- **Comments:** on, sorted by *Top*. **Remixing:** allowed. **Paid promotion:** no.
