This man threw away a hard drive. At its peak, it was worth about a billion dollars.
In 2009, James Howells, a computer engineer in Wales, mined eight thousand bitcoins on his laptop. They were worth almost nothing.
He kept the keys on an old hard drive, in a drawer.
In 2013, while he was clearing out the house, it went into a bin bag by mistake, and the bag went to the dump.
Months later, Bitcoin exploded, and he realized what he'd thrown away.
He begged the council to let him dig. They said no.
He offered them a quarter of the fortune. He brought investors, AI scanners, even robot dogs. Still no.
And the price kept climbing.
He sued the council for almost five hundred million pounds. The judge threw it out.
He even offered to buy part of the dump. The council plans to close it for good.
After twelve years, he gave up.
The hard drive is still down there. Would you have kept digging?
