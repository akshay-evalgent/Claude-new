# Xu Xiang: research sheet

Compiled 17 Nov 2026. Status of each fact:

- **Court / official**: stated by a court, Xinhua or a regulator.
- **Reported**: reported by a named outlet, often from unnamed sources.
- **Legend**: rumour or unproven claim. May be told only as such.
- **Check**: used in the script but not yet confirmed to the standard above.
