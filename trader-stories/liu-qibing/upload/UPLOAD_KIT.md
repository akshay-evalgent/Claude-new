## Description

```
London, November 2005. Copper is at a record high, and a rumour is moving the market: a trader working for the Chinese government has promised to deliver well over a hundred thousand tonnes of copper he does not have. And he has vanished. Asked about him, Beijing first says it does not know who he is.

This is the true story of Liu Qibing, the star trader of China's State Reserve Bureau: the man who built the bureau's computer link to the London Metal Exchange, made the state a fortune on China's building boom, then bet that the boom was over. His paper loss reached $606 million. Copper went on to nearly $9,000. And in 2008, it crashed below the price he had sold at.
```
