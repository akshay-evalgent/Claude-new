# Ginzō Korekawa (是川銀蔵): research sheet

Compiled 18 Nov 2026. Most sources are Japanese; no contemporary English-language
press was found. Status of each fact:

- **Reference**: in a published biographical dictionary or a government source.
- **Reported**: in a named publication or the foundation's own biography.
- **Book only**: in Japanese Wikipedia, citing a biography; not seen elsewhere. Use with care.
- **Conflict**: sources disagree; the script avoids the disputed number.

Nickname: 最後の相場師, "the last speculator" (KB).

His trading rules are widely summarised as the "three turtle principles" (pick
stocks yourself, not on tips; forecast the economy years ahead; never chase a
price), but only secondary summaries were found. The script paraphrases them and
attributes them to "as later writers summed them up".

## Money in context

- ¥20 billion at 1982 rates (about ¥249 to the dollar) is roughly $80 million.
- No reliable conversion was found for ¥70 in 1931, so the script gives none.
