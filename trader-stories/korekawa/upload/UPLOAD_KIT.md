## Description

```
In September 1981, an 84-year-old man in Osaka read a short newspaper item about gold found under a hill in Kagoshima. Millions of people read the same paper. Within months he had made a reported ¥20 billion, and the next year he topped Japan's list of taxpayers. Ten years later, he said not a single sen of his stock profits remained.

This is the true story of Ginzō Korekawa, "the last speculator": a fisherman's son with an elementary school education who lost everything four times, taught himself economics in a library, started again with ¥70, and spent fifty years getting ready for one trade.
```
