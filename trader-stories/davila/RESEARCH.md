# Juan Pablo Dávila: research sheet

Compiled 18 Nov 2026. Most sources are Chilean; the English press record is the
1994 Wall Street Journal (via secondary summaries) and US regional papers. Each
fact has a status:

- **Official**: the Chilean Comptroller General's preliminary audit of 18 Feb 1994,
  sent to President Aylwin. It includes Codelco management's report to its board
  of 4 Feb 1994 as Annex 1.
- **Court**: a court ruling as reported by Chilean media.
- **Reported**: in a named publication.
- **Allegation**: claimed by Codelco in lawsuits, or by investigators; not a court
  finding. The script attributes these.
- **Conflict**: sources disagree; the script uses the official figure or a range.

So: copper had been falling for over a year when his "error" came in
September 1993, kept falling to a low in November, then turned. His short
positions lost money from November. Had he kept them open, 1994 would have
been far worse: copper rose another 65%.

## Conflicts handled in the script

- **The loss**: US$206m (Codelco, 4 Feb 1994, official) vs US$207m (WSJ; Codelco
  later), US$170-178m (later estimates). The script uses "about two hundred and
  six million dollars".
- **Mistake or fraud**: his account is a typing error; the court convicted him of
  fraud against the state and conflict-of-interest dealing. The script gives his
  account, then the investigation, then the verdicts, each attributed. Codelco's
  claims in its lawsuits are marked as allegations.
- **Total prison term**: reports differ (8 years in 2007 press); the script gives
  the Supreme Court's sentences, not a total.

## Not used
