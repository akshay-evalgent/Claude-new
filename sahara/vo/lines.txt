This man got lost in the Sahara Desert, for nine days. And he says he survived, by drinking bat blood.
In 1994, Italian police officer Mauro Prosperi was running the Marathon des Sables. A six-day race across the Moroccan desert.
On day four, a sandstorm hit. It raged for hours.
When it cleared, he was alone. He had run the wrong way.
He had only a little water left. Soon, it was gone.
Then, he found an abandoned shrine. Inside, the roof was covered in bats.
He caught them, and drank their blood to stay alive.
Then he kept walking. Only at dawn and dusk, to save his strength.
On the ninth day, he stumbled into a camp of nomads.
He was in Algeria. Almost three hundred kilometers off course.
He had lost around sixteen kilos, and it took him months to recover.
And four years later, he went back, and ran the race again.
