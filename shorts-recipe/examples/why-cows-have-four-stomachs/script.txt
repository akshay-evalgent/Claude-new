A cow eats every meal twice. It doesn't have four stomachs. It has one, with four rooms. Grass goes into the first, the rumen, a vat as big as a bathtub, packed with trillions of microbes that break down tough plant fiber. Then the cow burps the mush back up, and chews it again. That's chewing the cud, up to eight hours a day. The honeycomb room sorts it, the third squeezes out the water, and the fourth digests with acid, just like yours.
