## Description

```
A gecko could hang from your ceiling all day, with no glue at all. Look closer at its toes. They're lined with ridges, covered in about two million tiny hairs. Each hair splits into hundreds of even tinier tips. They're so small, they get close enough for the molecules themselves to pull together. A patch of hairs the size of a coin could lift a small child. To let go, a gecko simply peels its toes back, like tape, up to fifteen times a second.
```
