## Built-in engagement (from the playbook)

- **Loop:** the video ends on "…the more often" and restarts on "Your cat looks you dead in the eye…", on the same frame, with the purr running across the cut. Don't trim the ending or add an end screen; that breaks the loop.
- **Share prompt:** "Send this to someone whose cat does this" is on screen from 24.3 s to the end. It's also in the description.
- **Comment trigger:** the pinned comment above.

- Watch it a few times through on loop with sound. The mix and the loop seam were checked by measurement and transcription, not by ear.
