## Description

```
An octopus has three hearts, and blue blood. Two of the hearts sit by the gills, pumping blood through them to pick up oxygen. The third, the biggest, sends it around the body. That blood is blue because it carries oxygen on copper, not iron like ours. But here's the catch: every time an octopus jets through the water, that main heart pauses. That's why they'd rather crawl than swim. Swimming wears them out fast.

Send this to someone who hates swimming 🐙
```
