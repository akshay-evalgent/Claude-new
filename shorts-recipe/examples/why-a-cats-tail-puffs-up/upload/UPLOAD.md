## Description

The last line credits the 3D cats. They're CC BY 4.0, so **keep that line**: the licence requires it.

```
When a cat's tail puffs up like a bottle brush, it's the same thing that gives you goosebumps. Every hair grows from a tiny pit with its own tiny muscle. When the cat gets scared, adrenaline hits, and thousands of those muscles squeeze at once. The hairs stand straight up, and the tail can look twice as thick. Then the cat arches its back and turns sideways, so whatever scared it sees the biggest cat possible. Your goosebumps are left over from when our ancestors had fur.

Send this to someone whose cat puffs up 🐈
```
