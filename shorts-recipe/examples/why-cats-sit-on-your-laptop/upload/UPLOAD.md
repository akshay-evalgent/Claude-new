## Description

The last line credits the 3D cats. They're CC BY 4.0, so **keep that line**: the licence requires it.

```
Your cat isn't sitting on your laptop to annoy you. It's sitting on a heater. A laptop runs as warm as your skin, and cats feel best at temperatures that would make you sweat. Their ancestors lived in the desert. That's why it steals your warm seat, and follows the sunbeam across the floor. There's a second reason. The laptop is where your eyes are all day. To your cat, it's the thing stealing your attention, so it sits right on it. And the keys smell like your hands.

Send this to someone whose cat sits on their keyboard 💻
```
