## Description

```
That wet dog shake is a feat of physics. A soaked Labrador carries about a pound of water in its fur, and air-drying it would burn about a fifth of its daily food. So it shakes: about four times a second, head to tail, with its loose skin whipping the water off. A mouse? Thirty times a second.

Send this to someone who's been soaked by a wet dog 💦🐶
```
