## Description

```
Flamingos aren't born pink. Their chicks hatch fluffy and grey. The pink comes from their food: tiny shrimp and algae full of orange-red pigments called carotenoids, the same kind that make carrots orange. A flamingo's body pulls out those pigments and packs them into its growing feathers, legs and beak. Take them away, and new feathers grow in pale, which is why zoos add pigment to their food. And in mating season, flamingos even wear makeup, rubbing pink oil from a gland near their tail all over their feathers.
```
