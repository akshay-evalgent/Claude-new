## Description

```
Brain freeze isn't in your brain. Cold on the roof of your mouth makes the blood vessels clamp shut and then flood open, and a nerve that also covers your forehead takes the blame. The fix: press your tongue against the roof of your mouth.

Send this to someone who eats ice cream way too fast 🍦

#shorts #science #brainfreeze #humanbody
```
