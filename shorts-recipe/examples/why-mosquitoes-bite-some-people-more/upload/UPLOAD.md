## Description

```
Some people really are mosquito magnets. Only females bite: they need blood to make eggs. First, she follows your breath, the carbon dioxide, from up to thirty feet away. Up close, she smells your skin. Your skin makes oils, and bacteria turn them into your own personal scent. In one study, people wore nylon stockings on their arms, and the mosquitoes picked one person a hundred times more than another. That person's skin made far more of certain acids, and it stayed that way for years.

Send this to someone who always gets bitten 🦟
```
