## Description

```
That growl isn't your stomach begging for food. Between meals, about every hour and a half, a wave of muscle sweeps through your stomach and intestines to clear out leftovers. With no food inside, it pushes only gas and liquid, and the bubbles echo like water in old pipes. Doctors call it borborygmi. Your gut rumbles after meals too, but food muffles the sound, and hunger hormones can set the wave off early.

Send this to someone whose stomach growls in exams 😳🔊
```
