When you crack your knuckles, that loud pop isn't your bones at all. You see, every finger joint sits in a tiny capsule filled with slippery fluid, and when you pull it apart, the pressure drops so fast that a gas bubble suddenly bursts into existence. Scientists filmed it happening inside an MRI. And that bubble takes about twenty minutes to dissolve, which is why you can't crack the same knuckle again right away. But does it cause arthritis?
