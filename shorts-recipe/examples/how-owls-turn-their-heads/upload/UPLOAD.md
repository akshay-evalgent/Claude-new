## Description

```
An owl can turn its head almost all the way around: 270 degrees. It has to, because its eyes aren't balls like ours. They're long tubes, locked in place, so the head does the looking. That twist would tear the blood vessels in a human neck. But an owl has fourteen neck bones, twice as many as us, its arteries run through holes ten times wider than they are, cushioned with air, and under the jaw they swell into little pools of blood that keep the brain fed while the head turns.

Send this to someone with a stiff neck 🦉
```
