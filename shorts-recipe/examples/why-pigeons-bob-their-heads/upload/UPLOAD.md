## Description

```
That pigeon isn't bobbing its head. With every step, its head locks still in the air while its body walks forward underneath, then snaps ahead to catch up. Hold, snap. A pigeon can barely move its eyes, so it steadies its whole head instead, like a camera on a tripod, and gets a sharp, still picture to spot seeds and hawks. Scientists showed it with a treadmill: when the world around a walking pigeon stopped moving, so did the bobbing.

Send this to someone who walks like a pigeon 🐦
```
