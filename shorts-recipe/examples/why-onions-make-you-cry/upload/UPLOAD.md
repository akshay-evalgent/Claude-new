## Description

Onions make you cry on purpose. Cutting one mixes two chemicals that its cells keep apart, and an enzyme turns them into a gas that stings your eyes, so they flood with tears to wash it out. Scientists found that exact enzyme in 2002. The fix: chill the onion first, and use a sharp knife.

Send this to someone who cries over onions 🧅😢
