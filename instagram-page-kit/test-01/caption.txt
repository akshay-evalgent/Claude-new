Most people quit right before it starts working. 🌱⏳
⠀
A savings account that barely moves. A skill that feels useless for months. A page with 40 followers. A business nobody has heard of yet. At the start nothing seems to happen, so it's easy to decide it isn't working.
⠀
But growth almost never arrives in a straight line. The roots go down first, where nobody can see them. Then one day the shoot breaks through, and everyone calls it an overnight success.
⠀
Money works the same way. $300 a month grows to about $55,000 in 10 years, and to roughly $447,000 in 30 (assuming 8% a year, which isn't guaranteed). The later years do most of the work, and you only get them if you stay.
⠀
So keep watering things, even when there's nothing to show for it yet.
⠀
Slow is still growing. Sudden is just slow that nobody noticed.
