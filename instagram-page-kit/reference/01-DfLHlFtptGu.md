## The hook

**"The Perfect Example Of What [Working Hard] Vs [Working Smart] Looks Like:"**

Why it works:
1. **It promises proof you can see.** "The perfect example of what X looks like" says: you don't need to read or listen, just watch.
2. **Two sides.** Everyone has an opinion on hard work vs smart work. The two yellow phrases are the two sides.
3. **The colon opens a question.** The only way to close it is to watch the clip.
4. **No context needed.** Anyone, anywhere, gets it in one second with the sound off.
5. **Title Case reads like a movie title card**, which fits the film clip.

## The caption (structure, summarised, not copied)

1. **A blunt claim:** hard work alone rarely builds wealth.
2. **A pattern people recognise:** people grind for years and stay in the same place.
3. **Two famous examples:** Jeff Bezos building systems that work without him (Amazon), and Warren Buffett's wealth mostly arriving through patience late in life.
4. **A two-line closer** that people screenshot: one line about hard work keeping you in the game, one about smart work deciding the winner.
5. **Credit and tags:** the film's name, then 5 hashtags: #Wealth #Mindset #Success #Strategy #Movies.

## What this changes in the kit

1. **@wealth is not only a business-news page.** Trackers call it "business, tech & news", but this reel (17.6M views) is a money-mindset lesson told through a movie scene. One reel isn't a pattern. 5 to 10 more links will show the real mix.
2. **The layout is simpler than my draft guessed.** There's no white headline box. It's centred Title Case text on black above a full-width clip. `scripts/headline.py --style metaphor` now reproduces it.
3. **The footage is a Hollywood film.** @wealth carries that risk at 19M followers. A new account shouldn't: the studio can mute, block or strike it, and a clip with a text overlay counts as unoriginal under Instagram's rules, which cuts off the recommendations a new account depends on. The kit keeps the hook, layout, length and caption structure, and swaps the footage for stock, public-domain, AI-made (labelled) or self-filmed scenes. See `content/07-MOVIE-METAPHOR-REELS.md`.
