Get 1% better every day and you end the year about 37 times better. 📈🧠
⠀
Get 1% worse every day and you end up with almost nothing. Same 365 days, same tiny step, opposite direction.
⠀
That's the part nobody feels in the moment. One skipped workout, one more thing you didn't need, one more hour of scrolling. Each day the difference is too small to notice, so it's easy to believe it doesn't matter.
⠀
But small things multiply. 1.01 to the power of 365 is 37.78. 0.99 to the power of 365 is 0.03. Money, skills and health all compound like this, in both directions.
⠀
You don't need a big change. You need a small one you repeat.
⠀
Tiny, daily, for a long time. That's the whole trick.
