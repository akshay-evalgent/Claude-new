# The recipe: animated reels built for 1M views in 24 hours and followers

For reels animated with Claude (motion graphics rendered from code) in the @wealth layout, with a song added inside Instagram. Every rule below is tied to the metric it moves and the maths in `09-VIRAL-MATH.md`.

**No recipe guarantees a million views.** From a normal first hour, 1M in a day means views doubling about every 2 hours for 24 hours. That only happens when a reel beats everything else Instagram could show those viewers. What the recipe does is raise the odds on every reel, and the volume rule at the end turns better odds into hits.

## The target in one line

```
(1 − skip rate) × completion × share rate  ≥  0.01      (an average reel scores 0.0012)
     ≥ 0.60            ≥ 0.70        ≥ 0.025
```
Hit all three and the reel sends about 8 to 10 times the signal of an average reel. Because the signals multiply, a 30% gain on each gives 2.2 times the total.

---

## 1. Skip rate → under 40%: win the first second

Skip rate is the share of viewers who swipe away in the first few seconds. The average is 60 to 65%.

| Rule | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Motion from frame 1.** No fade-in, logo or title card. Start mid-action: a counter already ticking, a shape already growing. | A still first frame looks like a photo, so the thumb keeps scrolling |
| **Hook of 7 words or fewer.** People read about 4 words a second, so 7 words take under 2 seconds. | If the hook takes longer to read than the decision to swipe, it doesn't count |
| **One object on screen to start.** One thing moving, high contrast, white or yellow on black. | The eye needs to know where to look in 0.3 seconds |
| **Open a question that only the end answers.** "1% Better Every Day 📈" makes you ask: how much better? | Curiosity is the only reason to stay that costs the viewer nothing |
| **A number in the hook or the first frame.** | Numbers stop thumbs. Every measured @wealth hook carries a concrete idea, and number hooks do best on money pages. |
| **Big text.** The hook at least 54 px on a 1080-wide frame, key words in yellow. | Text has to read on a phone held at arm's length, with the sound off |

## 2. Completion → over 70%: short, a progress bar, the payoff at the end, a loop

If you lose 40% in the first 3 seconds and then a few percent of the rest every second, here's what finishes:

| Length | Lose 2% a second | Lose 4% a second |
|---|---|---|
| 8 s | 54% | 49% |
| 12 s | 50% | 42% |
| 15 s | 47% | 37% |
| 30 s | 35% | 20% |
| 60 s | 19% | 6% |

Animations can't lean on story and faces the way @wealth's film clips do, so **keep them 8 to 15 seconds.**

| Rule | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **8 to 15 seconds.** | See the table: every extra second costs completion |
| **Show progress on screen:** a day counter, a filling bar, a timeline. | People stay to see a visible process finish |
| **The payoff in the last third**, never the start. | Give the answer early and the reason to stay is gone |
| **Something new every 1.5 to 2.5 seconds:** a number change, a new element, a camera push. | Resets attention before it drifts |
| **Seamless loop:** the last frame flows straight into the first. | Replays count as watch time. If 60% of finishers rewatch once, average watch time goes past 100% of the length. Animations loop easily; live footage rarely does. |
| **No end card, no "follow for more" slide.** | An outro is a guaranteed drop and breaks the loop |

## 3. Shares → over 2.5%: make it about someone else

Shares can only come from people who stayed:

```
shares per view = (1 − skip) × completion × chance a finisher shares
```
So everything above raises shares too. Then make the finisher think of a specific person:

| Share trigger | Animated example |
|---|---|
| **"This is so you"** (identity) | "Your money in your 20s vs 30s" |
| **"You need to see this"** (useful) | The rule of 72, 50/30/20, what $5 a day becomes |
| **"Whoa"** (surprising number) | 1% a day = 37x in a year |
| **"I told you so"** (settles an argument) | Renting vs buying, starting early vs late |

Add one send line to the caption that names the person: *"Send this to the friend who says they'll start next year."*

## 4. Likes → over 4%: an "aha" plus a satisfying motion

- **The reveal moment** (the big number landing) needs a physical punch: scale it up 10 to 15% and settle back, with a short hold.
- **Smooth, satisfying motion.** Ease in and out, no jitter. Satisfying motion gets liked like satisfying videos do.
- **Make the song's drop land on the reveal.** In Instagram's music picker, slide the song's start so its drop or beat change hits the reveal second. Each reel's README says when that second is.
- @wealth's measured like rates: 4.0% to 8.3%. The most emotional reel had the highest.

## 5. Followers → views × profile visits × follow rate

```
1M views × 1% visit the profile × 30% follow  =  3,000 followers
1M views × 2% visit the profile × 40% follow  =  8,000 followers
```
These rates are illustrative, not published benchmarks. Raise both:

| Lever | How |
|---|---|
| **More profile visits** | Name a series on screen and in the caption: "Money Maths #3". Viewers go to the profile looking for #1 and #2. |
| **More profile visits** | Same look every time: black frame, yellow highlight, same font. A reel that's recognisably yours makes people curious about the page. |
| **Higher follow rate** | Bio promises what's next: "One money lesson a day, in 15 seconds." |
| **Higher follow rate** | Grid of 9+ reels in the same style, best 3 pinned. Visitors follow pages that look like they'll keep delivering. |
| **Higher follow rate** | Caption follow line that states the value: "Follow for the maths nobody taught you at school ⭐" |
| **Second wave** | When a reel takes off, post part 2 within 24 hours. New visitors find a fresh reel and follow. |

## 6. Volume: how better odds turn into a million

If each reel has chance *p* of reaching 1M, the chance that at least one of *n* reels does is `1 − (1 − p)ⁿ`:

| Chance per reel | 10 reels | 30 reels | 60 reels | 100 reels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5% | 5% | 14% | 26% | 39% |
| 1% | 10% | 26% | 45% | 63% |
| 2% | 18% | 45% | 70% | 87% |

The recipe raises *p*. Volume is what cashes it in. Animations made with Claude are cheap to produce, so **1 to 2 a day for 30 to 60 days** is realistic. @wealth has 12,690 posts.

## 7. The 24-hour launch protocol

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| Before posting | Score the reel on the checklist below. Under 8 of 10: fix it first. |
| Posting | 7 to 9 pm your audience's time. Trending song, drop placed on the reveal. Caption with send line and series name. 3 to 5 specific hashtags. |
| 0 to 1 h | Reply to every comment with a real answer. Don't share it to friends. |
| 1, 3, 6, 12 h | Note total views. Run `python3 scripts/viral_math.py track 1:… 3:… 6:…`. |
| Growing by 25%+ an hour | Have part 2 ready. Pin the reel. Share it to your story. |
| Flat or fading | Leave it up. Change one thing on the next reel (hook, length or reveal). |

## Pre-flight checklist (score before every post)

| # | Check | Point |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Something is moving on frame 1 | 1 |
| 2 | Hook is 7 words or fewer, with a number or contrast | 1 |
| 3 | One clear focal object at the start | 1 |
| 4 | Length 8 to 15 s | 1 |
| 5 | Visible progress (counter, bar, timeline) | 1 |
| 6 | Payoff in the last third, with a punch | 1 |
| 7 | Something new every 2.5 s or less | 1 |
| 8 | Seamless loop, no outro | 1 |
| 9 | You can name who a viewer would send it to | 1 |
| 10 | Series name, and the same look as the rest of the grid | 1 |

## The production line

```
1. Pick the idea (a money or mindset truth with a number in it).
2. Ask Claude to animate it with scripts/make_anim_reel.py (one scene function per idea).
3. Claude renders it, checks the frames and the loop, and writes the caption.
4. You post it with a trending song, drop on the reveal second.
5. You send back the 1, 3, 6, 12 h views. Claude reads the trend and adjusts the next one.
```
