# Growth playbook: how Instagram picks who sees your reels, the daily routine, and the first 90 days

## A realistic view of "millions of views"

Reels are shown mostly to people who don't follow you, so a brand-new account can get a million views on one reel. It happens to business pages every week. But it isn't predictable. Here is what usually happens:

- **Weeks 1 to 2:** most reels get a few hundred views. Instagram is working out who your audience is. This is normal, not a sign that something is broken.
- **Weeks 3 to 6:** one reel in ten or so does many times your usual views. That's the signal to study.
- **Months 2 to 3:** if you've posted every day and doubled down on what worked, breakouts get more frequent and your typical views go up.

Millions of views come from a few outliers on top of a lot of consistent posting. The plan below is built to give you as many outliers as possible.

## How Instagram decides who sees a reel (what's confirmed)

From Adam Mosseri, head of Instagram, in 2025 and 2026 statements:

1. **The top three signals are watch time, likes and sends.** Likes count a bit more with followers. **Sends count more with non-followers**, and that's the audience you need.
2. **More and more reach comes from recommendations**, meaning people who don't follow you.
3. **Originality matters.** Reposted content gets replaced by the original in recommendations, and accounts that mostly repost can stop being recommended. See `06-ORIGINALITY-AND-SOURCING.md`.
4. **Other apps' watermarks reduce distribution.** Content made elsewhere is fine if the file is clean.
5. **Comments count, but they aren't the main signal.** Sends are.
6. **Carousels can come back.** Instagram can show them to the same person again.
7. **Hashtags help with search, not reach.** Use 3 to 5 specific ones. Sources disagree on whether Instagram now caps hashtags at 5, so stay at 5 or fewer.
8. **Scheduling posts doesn't hurt reach.** Neither does deleting a flop. Ads don't change organic reach.
9. **AI content isn't punished for being AI, but label it** when it looks or sounds real.
10. **Trial reels** show a reel to non-followers first. Several in one day is fine. Posting the same trial reel again and again is not.

### The three numbers to watch

Open each reel's Insights 48 hours after posting and log these in a spreadsheet:

| Metric | How to get it | What it tells you | Working target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Skip rate** | Reel Insights | Did the hook work? | Below your average and falling |
| **Average watch time ÷ length** | Reel Insights | Did the rest hold attention? | Above 60% for reels under 20 s |
| **Sends per reach** | Shares ÷ Accounts reached | Did people pass it on? This is the one that matters most. | 1% is good, 3%+ usually means a breakout |

Also log likes ÷ reach and follows from the reel. Ignore total views for the first month. They're mostly luck while Instagram is still learning about the account.

The targets are working benchmarks from what business pages report, not official Instagram numbers. Your own averages matter more. Beat yesterday's.

### Diagnosis

| What you see | What it means | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| High skip rate | Hook failed | Rewrite the headline. Start on the most interesting frame. Cut the first second. |
| Low skip rate, low watch time | Hook fine, middle sags | Cut 30% of the script. B-roll change every 2 s. Move the payoff earlier. |
| Good watch time, few sends | Interesting but not personal | Pick topics people tie to someone they know ("your friend who loves Costco"). Add a line worth repeating. |
| Lots of sends, few follows | Good reel, weak profile | Fix bio, pins and highlights so visitors see what else you post |

## Writing for sends

Before you make a reel, finish this sentence: **"People will send this to ___ because ___."**

If you can't fill the blanks, pick another topic. Topics people send:

- "I told you so" facts: proof of something people argue about (rent vs buy, college vs no college).
- Brand secrets about places everyone goes: Costco, McDonald's, IKEA, Starbucks, Amazon.
- Money maths that makes people feel urgency, or relief.
- News that affects a job or a wallet: layoffs, price changes, AI replacing tasks.
- Founder stories with a humble start, for the friend who's starting something.

Put the send prompt in the caption when it fits naturally: "Send this to the friend who still thinks McDonald's is a burger company."

## Daily routine (90 minutes)

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 15 min | **Scan the news.** Reuters business, Bloomberg and CNBC headlines, TechCrunch, The Verge, Google News "Business", company newsrooms, X/Threads trending. Write down 5 stories that pass the send test. |
| 5 min | **Pick 2.** One news (F1/F6), one evergreen (F2/F4/F5 from the 30-reel list). |
| 10 min | **Check the facts.** Every number from two independent sources. Note the sources for the caption. |
| 40 min | **Make the reels.** Headline PNG with `scripts/headline.py`, VO on phone, edit in Edits or CapCut with your saved template. |
| 5 min | **Caption and cover.** Template in `01-WEALTH-TEARDOWN.md`. |
| 15 min | **First hour after posting:** reply to every comment with something real. Ask a follow-up question. Pin the best comment. |

Post at consistent times. Check Insights > Total followers > Most active times after week 2. Until then, 8 to 9 AM and 6 to 8 PM in the time zone where most of your audience lives.

## The first 90 days

### Days 1 to 3: launch
- Set up the profile (`02-PROFILE-SETUP.md`).
- Post the 9-post launch grid.
- Same handle on TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Post the same clean files there.

### Days 4 to 30: find the format
- **Volume:** 1 reel a day minimum, 2 when you can. Use trial reels for the second one when your account gets the toggle.
- **Test all seven formats** at least three times each.
- **Test hooks:** for your best topics, post a trial reel with a different headline and compare skip rates.
- Log every reel in a spreadsheet.
- **Day 30 review:** sort by sends per reach. Which two formats and which three topics come out on top?

### Days 31 to 60: find the series
- Turn your best topic type into a **named series** with a number: "How it makes money #14". Series bring people back and make bingeing easy.
- Raise to 2 posts a day, weighted to your top two formats.
- **Remake your top 5 reels** with new hooks and a fresh edit. Not the same file. A remake is new work, so it counts as original.
- Start **collabs**: use Instagram's Collab feature with similar-sized pages in money, career or tech. Both audiences see the reel.
- Launch the **newsletter** (beehiiv or Substack, free). Add a keyword offer: "Comment BRIEF and I'll send you this week's 5 stories." Reply by DM yourself at first. Use an automation tool later.

### Days 61 to 90: scale and get ready to make money
- When a reel breaks out (10x your median), post a **part 2 within 24 hours**, pin it, share it to stories, and make a carousel version.
- Build a **media kit**: follower count, average reach, top countries, age split, 3 best reels with screenshots of their numbers.
- From about **10K followers**, reach out to sponsors in finance and software (see below).
- Re-check this playbook against your tracker. Kill what isn't working.

## When a reel breaks out

1. Reply to as many comments as you can in the first 6 hours. Breakouts often get a second wave.
2. Share it to your story with a question sticker.
3. Post a follow-up on the same topic within 24 hours. People who just followed want more of exactly that.
4. Pin it.
5. Make the carousel version within the week.
6. Write down what was different: topic, headline wording, first frame, length. That's your formula until something beats it.

## Making money

Rough order in which each becomes possible:

| Stage | Income | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Any size | Affiliate links for tools you actually use (software, books, broker sign-up bonuses) | Disclose clearly. In the US that's FTC rules, in India ASCI, in the UK ASA. Use Instagram's Paid partnership label for paid posts. |
| 5K+ engaged followers | Small sponsored posts from AI tools and apps | Ask for a flat fee plus affiliate. Licensed audio only on sponsored posts. |
| 10K to 50K | Regular sponsors, newsletter sponsorships | Finance and business audiences command some of the higher rates per follower |
| 50K+ | Instagram's own monetization programs where available in your country (subscriptions, gifts, bonuses), your own digital product (a money spreadsheet, a startup guide) | Check Professional dashboard > Monetization status |

**Never promote:** guaranteed-return schemes, "trading signals", crypto presales, anyone who DMs you with a "partnership" that pays per sign-up for an investment app you've never heard of. One scam sponsor costs more trust than a year of posts earns. Check that any financial product you promote is regulated where your audience lives.

## Things that waste time

- Hashtag research beyond 3 to 5 relevant tags.
- Posting at the "perfect" minute.
- Engaging with random accounts before posting (Mosseri has called this a myth).
- Deleting reels that flop to "clean up" the grid. It doesn't change reach. Archive if they bother you.
- Waiting until it's perfect. Reel #40 will be better than reel #1 no matter what. Get to #40.
