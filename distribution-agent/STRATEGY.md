# Strategy: positioning, business model and first clients

Written 18 November 2026. Decision taken: serve international makers first. This file turns the research in this folder into what we sell, to whom, for how much, and how we find them.

## How far to trust this

- Nothing here is tested. Prices, reply rates and client counts are starting points to check against the first sprints.
- Reply and conversion estimates come from datasets of founders who succeeded and wrote about it.
- No account was found of an India-based agency winning foreign clients through LinkedIn or YouTube. US prospects replied least (6%) in one outreach study.
- How an India-based service business best collects payment from foreign clients was not researched. See section 9.
- Competitor prices come partly from competitors' own blogs.

| Question | Decision |

Not served:

## 3. Where competitors lag

| Group | How they charge | Where it falls short |
|---|---|---|
| AI growth tools (Okara, Revnu, Hoox, XGrowth) | $99–249 a month, often credits | Charge founders with no revenue, for activity. Polsia's founder reports about 50% month-one churn |
| Open-source skills (marketingskills, gtm-cofounder) | Free, then consulting | Need a coding agent; nobody does the work |
| Intent finders (Prospy, Buska) | $24–249 a month | Hand over a list; the founder does the rest |
| Auto-posters (Rankcow, Leadmore) | $99–199 a month or per action | Post from rented accounts, which Reddit bans for |
| Agencies and sprints | $2,500–9,500, prices often hidden | Serve funded B2B |
| Agent marketplaces (MCPize, Apify) | 15–20% of sales | A listing and billing, not customers |

Across all of them:

- Almost none publishes how many users got a paying customer.
- None does store listings for the client.
- Almost none serves sellers of templates, courses or ebooks.
- None sits between $99 tools and $2,500 agencies.

## 4. The proposition

**Headline:** Your first buyer conversations and a live store listing in 14 days. Fixed price. Results published.

**Supporting line:** You built it. We find the 30 people already asking for it, put your product where buyers search, and coach every reply. You send the messages; we do the rest.

Why a founder should believe it:

| Proof | What it is |
|---|---|
| The free report | Real buyer conversations for their own product, before they pay anything |
| Diagnosis first | We decline the sprint when the problem is demand or positioning |
| Done for you | The store listing, the conversation list and the opening messages |
| Public scorecard | Every sprint's conversations, signups and sales, published with permission |
| Refund | Money back if any deliverable is missed |
| No ban risk | Nothing is posted by a bot or from a rented account |

Why we can offer it at this price: our cost base is in India. A US agency cannot run a hands-on sprint for $399.

## 5. How we serve them

### The free report

A founder's URL goes in. They get back:

- The one narrow customer to target.
- 10–20 live posts where people describe that problem, with links.
- The one channel or marketplace to use.
- One opening message per post, for the founder to edit and send.

### The 14-day sprint

| Days | What we do | What the founder does |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Intake by form or call. Diagnosis memo: one buyer, one problem, demand check. Refund in full if demand is absent | Answer the intake |
| 3–5 | Choose one marketplace and build the listing. Write the one-page offer and price recommendation. Set up tracking with a "how did you hear about us" field | Approve and submit the listing |
| 3–10 | Find 30 people who stated the problem in the last 30 days. Draft an opening line for each. Coach replies daily | Send messages and reply, about 30 minutes a day |
| 8–12 | Follow-ups. Draft the paid pilot offer. Summarise why non-buyers said no | Ask for payment |
| 14 | Scorecard and a 30-day plan naming the one channel to keep | Approve the scorecard for publishing |

Guaranteed deliverables:

1. Diagnosis memo.
2. One listing built and submitted.
3. 30 qualified conversations with opening lines.
4. Offer page copy and a price recommendation.
5. Tracking in place.
6. Scorecard and 30-day plan.

### The retainer

- 20 new qualified conversations a week.
- Listing upkeep and reviews.
- Comparison pages and tutorial scripts.
- Interviews with customers who left.
- A weekly report against signups and payments.
- Paid channels only after the gate in `PAID-AND-SOCIAL-CHANNELS-PLAYBOOK.md` is passed.

### Working across time zones

- Delivery is written and asynchronous by default.
- One call slot a week in the Indian evening, which is the US morning.
- Replies to client messages within one working day.

## 7. How we get international clients

### Where to find founders who have said they need this

| Source | Signal |
|---|---|
| TrustMRR listings | 58% show zero recurring revenue, with the founder named |
| Reddit: r/SaaS, r/indiehackers, r/SideProject | Posts about no users or no sales |
| Indie Hackers | Same |
| X | Founders posting launch results |
| Product Hunt, Show HN, Peerlist, Uneed | Recent launches with few votes |
| Shopify App Store | New apps with no reviews; a forum audit put this at 80% of 2026 launches |
| Chrome Web Store, MCP registry | New listings with few installs |
| Gumroad, Etsy, Framer | New sellers with no sales signal |

### The outreach

1. Pick 10 founders a day by hand.
2. Build each one's report.
3. Send it where they already are: a public reply where the community allows it, otherwise a direct message or an email from their site.
4. Offer the sprint only to those who reply.

Rules:

- A person sends every message.
- Lists stay under 50 at a time. Small hand-picked lists got 14–58% replies; scraped lists got 1–1.6%.
- Two to three weeks of ordinary participation in a community before any mention of the service.
- Email to the US needs a postal address and an opt-out.
- For Germany and Austria use direct messages or public replies, not cold email.

### Channels, in order

| Channel | Use | Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| Reports sent unasked | Main channel from day 1 | 50 reports may bring 7–29 replies and 2–5 sprints; expect the low end for US prospects |
| Referrals | Ask every sprint client for one introduction | Top source of new business for most agencies |
| LinkedIn | 4–5 founder posts a week showing real reports; about 100 hand-picked invites a week | 4–15 calls in 90 days; inbound unlikely before month 3 |
| Public scorecards | Publish each sprint's result as a post | Proof for every other channel |
| YouTube | Screen recordings of building a report for a real product | Slow; under 1,000 subscribers in year one is the base case |
| Borrowed lists | Offer free reports to the audience of a boilerplate seller, launch platform or newsletter | One documented case |
| Ads | None yet | A client from LinkedIn ads costs about $3,000–6,000 |

### Overcoming distance

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "I do not know you" | The free report is real work on their own product |
| "Will you spam from my account?" | We never log in or post; they send everything |
| "What if it does not work?" | Refund on any missed deliverable; public scorecards |
| "Time zones" | Written delivery, one weekly call slot |

## 9. Open items to check before selling

| Item | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Payment route for a service business in India billing foreign clients | Direct Stripe is invite-only. Merchant-of-record platforms may not allow human services. PayPal costs about 7–8% all-in; Razorpay charges up to 3% plus GST on international cards |
| Tax paperwork for exported services | Have a chartered accountant confirm GST and the export undertaking |
| Affiliate split on the client's checkout | Gumroad has one built in; Polar and Dodo are unconfirmed |
| Each marketplace's rule on third parties preparing listings | We build; the founder submits from their own account |
| Sprint capacity per person | Sets how many sprints we can sell a month |

## 10. What we do not do

- Autonomous posting or rented accounts.
- A generic AI CMO subscription.
- Developer-tool GTM skills as a product; `gtm-cofounder` is already there.
- AI-answer tracking as a product.
- Any promise of revenue.
