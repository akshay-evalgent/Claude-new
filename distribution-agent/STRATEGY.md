# Strategy: positioning, business model and first clients

Updated 18 November 2026. Decisions taken: the premium line for founders who build with AI is the main focus, sold as a one-time Distribution Setup followed by a Distribution Partner retainer with a flat option and a growth option. This file is the top-level summary. `AI-BUILDER-MODEL.md` holds the detail of each offer.

## How far to trust this

- Nothing here is tested. Prices, reply rates and client counts are starting points to check against the first clients.
- No research was done on what this segment will pay. The premium case rests on judgement and on prices of comparable human services.
- Reply and conversion estimates come from datasets of founders who succeeded and wrote about it.
- No account was found of an India-based agency winning foreign clients through LinkedIn or YouTube. US prospects replied least (6%) in one outreach study.
- How an India-based service business best collects payment from foreign clients was not researched. See section 9.
- The revenue-share terms have not been reviewed by a lawyer.
- Competitor prices come partly from competitors' own blogs.

Qualifying questions before any paid step:

1. Do you earn a living in the industry this product serves?
2. Does a customer pay at least $300 a year?
3. Can a stranger sign up, pay and get a useful result today?
4. Can you give 30–45 minutes a day for 30 days?

Not served:

- Idea-stage founders with nothing live.
- Trading, investment-tip or income-claim products.
- Founders who want us to log in to their personal accounts.

## 3. Where competitors lag

| Group | How they charge | Where it falls short |
|---|---|---|
| Building platforms (Lovable) | Marketing features planned for 2027 | Nothing today |
| Self-serve agents (Morket, NowBuild, AgentCeres, Okara) | Free to $249 a month | Assume the founder will do the marketing |

Across all of them:

- Almost none publishes how many users got a paying customer.
- None does store listings for the client.
- None starts from the founder's own trade network.
- Nobody found sells a fixed-price setup for AI-built products with published results.

## 4. The proposition

**Headline:** You built it with AI. We build the distribution.

**Supporting line:** In 30 days we fix your offer, set up your channels, and line up the first 80 conversations with people who have the problem. You pay half to start and half on delivery.

| Proof | What it is |
|---|---|
| The free report | Real buyer conversations for their own product, before they pay anything |
| Week-1 verdict | Go, change or stop. On "stop" we refund $700 |
| Done for you | The offer page, the channels, the posts, the listings, the network map and every opening message |
| Half on delivery | The second $500 is due only when the setup is delivered |
| Public scorecard | Each client's conversations, signups and sales, published with permission |
| No ban risk | Nothing is posted by a bot or from a rented account |

Why we can offer it at this price: our cost base is in India. Comparable human services start at $2,500.

## 5. How we serve them

### What we publish and what they send

| Where | Who acts | How |
|---|---|---|
| Company page, YouTube channel, website, blog | We write and schedule; they approve | Page roles or a scheduler they authorise |
| Store and directory listings | We build; they submit from their account | — |
| Community replies on Reddit, Hacker News, forums | They send | We find the thread and draft the reply |
| Personal messages and their personal LinkedIn profile | They send or approve | We draft; we never log in |

### Distribution Setup, 30 days

| Week | We deliver | Founder does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verdict: go, change or stop. Positioning. Six-point readiness check | Answer the intake |
| 2 | Offer page rewritten. Price and trial set. Tracking. One main and one support channel chosen and set up | Approve the page; grant channel roles |
| 3 | 12 posts written and scheduled. Two listings. 50 people from their own network and 30 public conversations, each with an opening message | Approve posts; submit listings; start sending |
| 4 | Daily reply coaching. Scorecard. 90-day plan | Send, reply, ask for payment |

A "stop" verdict in week 1 ends the work: we keep $300 and refund $700.

### Distribution Partner, monthly

- Weekly posts for their channels, written and scheduled for approval.
- 20 new conversations a week, with opening messages.
- Continued network outreach.
- Listing upkeep and review requests.
- Comparison pages and tutorial scripts.
- Interviews with customers who leave.
- A weekly report against signups and sales.
- Paid channels only after the gate in `PAID-AND-SOCIAL-CHANNELS-PLAYBOOK.md` is passed.

We offer the partner step only when the setup has produced a channel that is getting replies.

### Rules for delivery

- The method starts with the founder's own trade network, then public conversations.
- One main channel and one support channel. No more.
- We check readiness and list fixes. We do not fix code or review security, and we say so in writing.
- Delivery is written and asynchronous, with one call slot a week in the Indian evening, which is the US morning.

The tool builds the report, runs the readiness check and logs messages and sales. It is free for clients.

## 7. How we get clients

### Where to find founders who built with AI

| Place | Signal |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Posts saying "I built an app with AI" by people with a non-engineering job title |
| Lovable, Bolt, Replit and v0 showcases and "made with" badges | Live apps with a named builder |
| Product Hunt | First-time makers; 61% of AI launchers were first-timers in mid-2026 |
| X | "Built with Lovable" or "built with Cursor" launch posts |
| Reddit communities for AI building | Posts asking how to get users |
| TrustMRR | Listings with no revenue |
| YouTube | Comments on "build an app with AI" tutorials asking what comes next |

These places need checking by hand.

### The outreach

1. Pick 10 founders a day by hand and run the four qualifying questions against what is public.
2. Build each one's report.
3. Send it where they already are: a public reply where allowed, otherwise a direct message or an email.
4. Offer a call to those who reply.
5. Offer the setup on the call. Offer the partner step at the end of the setup.

Rules:

- A person sends every message.
- Lists stay under 50 at a time. Small hand-picked lists got 14–58% replies; scraped lists got 1–1.6%.
- Email to the US needs a postal address and an opt-out.
- For Germany and Austria use direct messages or public replies, not cold email.
- One follow-up, then stop.

### Channels, in order

| Channel | Use | Expectation |
|---|---|---|
| Reports sent unasked | Main channel from day 1 | 50 reports may bring 7–29 replies; expect the low end for US prospects |
| LinkedIn | Where this segment posts. 4–5 founder posts a week showing real reports; about 100 hand-picked invites a week | 4–15 calls in 90 days; inbound unlikely before month 3 |
| Referrals | Ask every client for one introduction in their trade | Top source of new business for most agencies |
| Public scorecards | Publish each client's result | Proof for every other channel |
| YouTube | Screen recordings of reviewing a real AI-built product | Slow; under 1,000 subscribers in year one is the base case |
| Borrowed lists | Offer free reports to the audience of an AI-building educator or community | One documented case of this pattern |
| Ads | None yet | A client from LinkedIn ads costs about $3,000–6,000, which only the partner fee recovers |

### Overcoming distance

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "I do not know you" | The free report is real work on their own product |
| "$1,000 is a lot" | Half to start, half on delivery; $700 back if we advise stopping |
| "Will you spam from my account?" | We publish only to their own channels, with approval; they send everything personal |
| "What if it does not work?" | Refund for missed deliverables; public scorecards |
| "A share of my revenue?" | Optional. Growth only, capped, and it ends three months after we stop |
| "Time zones" | Written delivery, one weekly call slot |

## 9. Open items to check before selling

| Item | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Payment route for a service business in India billing foreign clients | Direct Stripe is invite-only. Merchant-of-record platforms may not allow human services. PayPal costs about 7–8% all-in; Razorpay charges up to 3% plus GST on international cards |
| Whether this segment pays $1,000 | Untested. Send 50 reports before building more |
| Revenue-share agreement | Needs a lawyer's check, and clients must agree to dashboard access |
| Each platform's rules for third-party page roles and schedulers | Sets what we can publish for a client |
| Tax paperwork for exported services | Have a chartered accountant confirm GST and the export undertaking |
| Wording of the "no security review" note | Protects us when an AI-built product has flaws |
| Capacity per person | Sets how many setups and partners we can run a month |

## 10. What we do not do

- Autonomous posting or rented accounts.
- Logging in to a client's personal accounts.
- More than two channels per client.
- A generic AI CMO subscription.
- Code fixes or security reviews.
- A share of all revenue, or a share with no end date.
- Any promise of revenue.
