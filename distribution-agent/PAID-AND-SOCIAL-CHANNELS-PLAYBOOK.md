# LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube: organic and ads

Researched 11 November 2026. Covers two uses: landing our own service clients, and running these channels for clients who sell SaaS and digital products. Companion to `PLAN.md` and the other playbooks in this folder.

## How far to trust this

- Almost every cost benchmark comes from a vendor's own client panel. Those panels lean towards funded companies with large deals, not micro-SaaS or new agencies.
- Every published ad success case found was written by an agency selling that service.
- No agency published its full ad spend against retained client revenue.
- No first-person account was found of an Indian agency winning foreign clients through LinkedIn or YouTube.
- No first-person YouTube-ads write-up under $1,000 a month with full funnel numbers was found.
- No published benchmark exists for cost per SaaS trial on LinkedIn or YouTube. The figures here are arithmetic from click costs.
- Meta's help pages, Indie Hackers and Reddit could not be opened, so those items rest on secondary sources.
- The YouTube research ran out of search budget before covering production costs.
- The 90-day scenarios are arithmetic from the benchmarks, not observed outcomes.

## 1. The short answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Do ads on these platforms get clients? | Yes, for offers of $1,000–35,000 at spends of about $1,000 a day. Not shown at $500–2,000 a month |
| Best channel for landing our own clients | LinkedIn founder posts plus hand-picked invites, at no ad spend |
| Second | YouTube screen-recorded tutorials, as a slow asset |
| Lowest priority for us | Instagram |
| Closed to us in practice | TikTok |
| When ads make sense for us | Only to sell the retainer, after case studies exist |
| Where ads fit our tiers | Tier 2, and only for clients who pass the gate in section 7 |

## 2. Why it looks like everyone gets clients this way

- The visible funnel is an ad, then a free training or webinar, then an application, then a sales call.
- The profitable published cases sell $1,000–35,000 offers at $1,000 or more a day in ad spend.
- Referrals are the top source of new business for roughly 58–66% of agencies in two surveys. No survey found reports paid social as a meaningful source.
- One coaching client spent $40,000 on YouTube ads in 90 days for 200+ bookings, with show rates under 20% and almost no sales.
- Growth Cave used the same funnel to sell the business opportunity itself. It settled with the US Federal Trade Commission in January 2026 for $48.6M and was banned from selling business opportunities.

## 3. Channel by channel

### LinkedIn

Organic:

| Measure | Figure | Source and bias |
|---|---|---|
| Median founder post | About 500 impressions | 33 profiles, one vendor's clients |
| Share of impressions from top 5% of posts | 66% | Same |
| Audience growth from targeted invites | About half | Same |
| Best formats for reach | Polls 1.78x, documents 1.39x, images 1.20x, video 0.86x | 3M posts from one tool's users |
| Post containing a link | 13% fewer reactions | 116,000 posts |
| Best posting rate | 4–7 posts a week | Same 3M posts |
| Per 1,000 invites | 260 accept, 47 reply, 3–4 meetings | 500,000 invites, tool users |
| Time to inbound leads | 6–12 months in one agency's account | Self-reported |

- "Comment a keyword" posts went from 9% above the author's average reach to 52% below after late July 2026. LinkedIn names them as something it reduces.
- LinkedIn reported a 40% drop in views for content it classed as AI slop, and removed its own AI post-writer in August 2026.
- One founder's first 30 posts brought no leads. Post 31 brought 25 meetings and 3 clients.

Ads:

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|

### TikTok

- The app has been banned in India since June 2020.
- India is not on the eligible-country list for TikTok's new-advertiser incentive.
- TikTok Shop bars digital products except an approved US-only category.
- One ad group needs about $600 a month.
- Views per post fell 31% in early 2026 while video volume rose 72%.
- Views convert poorly: one video with about 5M views drove about $15,000; about 1M views gave another app $3,000.
- No verified case was found of short video converting to B2B SaaS signups.

### YouTube

Organic:

| Videos added in a year | Share of small channels reaching 1,000 subscribers |
|---|---|
| 0 | 0.2% |
| 1–11 | 1.9% |
| 12–51 | 8.7% |
| 52 or more | 30.8% |

- Source: 32.9M channels in one tool's index. Channels created after 2023 that reached 1,000 took a median of about 16 months.
- New channels cannot put clickable links in descriptions or pinned comments until they unlock advanced features by verification or history. Links in Shorts are never clickable.
- YouTube videos rank in Google's top 10 for 80% of SaaS "demo" keywords, where a click costs $20–49.
- YouTube's own director says the subscribe signal matters less than people think; new videos are shown to people who have never seen the channel.
- Shorts bring 6 in 10 views but no evidence of leads.

Ads:

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Minimum | $5 a day |
| Google's guidance | Daily budget of 10 times the target cost per conversion; about 50 conversions before judging |
| Floor named by Google's product manager | $100 a day |
| Cost per 1,000 impressions, US / emerging markets | $12.86 / $1.37 |
| Cost per booked call, one agency case | $152, for a $35,000+ offer |
| Webinar registration, one agency case | $4–6 |

- At $500 a month, Google's rule supports a target of about $1.70 per conversion. Booked calls cost $150 or more.
- Targeting specific competitor videos is no longer available in conversion campaigns.
- Remarketing lists need only 100 viewers.
- Buying subscribers with the Promote button brought about 12,000 low-quality subscribers for $300 in one test.

### Google Search, for comparison

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Business services, cost per click / per lead | $5.87 / $93.69 |
| What $500 buys | About 85 clicks and 4 leads |
| What $2,000 buys | About 340 clicks and 16 leads |
| B2B non-brand search, attributed return | 67–78% of spend |
| SaaS "demo" / "trial" / "alternative" keywords | About $49 / $37 / $33 a click |

Search wins when the buyer already types the need. One study put it as "YouTube introduces the brand; Google Search closes it."

### Attributed return for B2B, by platform

| Platform | Return on ad spend | Cost per lead |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 121% | $202 |
| Google Search | 67% | $524 |
| Meta | 51% | $138–145 |

Both figures come from attribution vendors' own customers. B2B journeys average 272 days from first ad touch to revenue.

## 5. Where each channel fits our three tiers

### Landing our own clients

| Channel | Use | When |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn organic | Founder posts 4–5 times a week showing real "First 10 Conversations" reports; about 100 hand-picked invites a week referencing the prospect's own post | Now |
| YouTube organic | Screen recordings of building a report for a real product, titled for what founders search | Now, as a slow asset |
| LinkedIn boosted posts | $300–500 a month behind the best-performing posts to a tight list; claim the matched credit | After the first case studies; read as a message test |
| LinkedIn lead-form ads | $25–50 a day | Only to sell the retainer |
| Instagram | Repost carousels as proof | Low priority |
| Meta, YouTube or Google ads | — | Not shown to pay back at our scale |
| TikTok | — | Not from India |

### Tier 1: free methods for clients

| Client | Free channel |
|---|---|
| Business SaaS | Founder LinkedIn posts plus hand-picked invites |
| Self-serve SaaS under $100 a month | YouTube tutorials on the exact tasks the product does |
| Creator tools and consumer apps | High-volume short video |
| Digital products | Instagram keyword-to-DM into an email list |

### Tier 2: paid channels for clients, by offer

| Client offer | Channel | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer or prosumer app with a hard paywall | TikTok and Meta ads | One profitable case at $18,000 a month |
| Free newsletter | Meta ads at $1.50–2 a subscriber | Agency cases |
| Paid community or course with a webinar step | Meta to webinar | 4.4% of leads became paid members in one case |
| Seller to Indian audiences | Meta lead forms and click-to-WhatsApp | ₹29–101 a lead |
| Business SaaS worth several thousand dollars a customer | LinkedIn lead forms and boosted founder posts; Google Search for buyers already searching | Best attributed B2B return |
| Service or course at $1,000+ | YouTube video to a call or webinar | Wins only at $30,000 a month or more |
| Self-serve SaaS under $100 a month | No ads; one affiliate deal with a small creator | Click costs cannot be recovered |
| $7–47 digital product to US or UK buyers | No ads unless a higher-priced follow-on offer exists | Loses on the first sale |

### Tier 3: human in the loop

- Draft LinkedIn posts for the founder to post. Logging in to a client's personal profile breaches LinkedIn's user agreement.
- Build and work the hand-picked invite lists.
- Script and edit screen-recorded tutorials.
- Run ad accounts through page and ad-account roles.

## 6. What this means for our pricing

- A client from LinkedIn ads costs roughly $3,000–6,000 to win at $2,000 a month in spend.
- The $299–499 sprint cannot recover that.
- A $1,500–2,500 a month retainer recovers it in two to four months if the client stays.
- So ads are for selling the retainer, and only once retention is known.

## 7. The gate for tier 2

Sell paid channels to a client only when all four hold:

1. The product already converts without ads, with a known visitor-to-paid rate.
2. A customer is worth more than the cost per customer on that channel.
3. The budget supports a real test: about $3,000–4,000 a month for at least 90 days.
4. Tracking is in place, including a "how did you hear about us" field.

Below that, the money is better spent on outreach time.

## 8. Our own first 90 days, by budget

| | $0 | About $500 a month | About $2,000 a month |
|---|---|---|---|
| What to do | Founder profile, 4–5 posts a week, about 100 hand-picked invites a week, daily comments; one tutorial video a week | The $0 plan plus boosted founder posts to a tight list | The $0 plan plus one LinkedIn lead-form campaign and boosted-post retargeting |
| Plausible calls | 4–15 | 1–4 extra | 5–12 extra |
| Plausible clients | 0–3 | 0–1 extra | 1–2 extra |
| Caveat | Inbound unlikely before month 3–6 | Below the level where results can be read | Half the level of a proper test |

## 9. Rules

- **Income claims:** Google bars unrealistic promises of financial return; Meta bars get-rich-quick ads; the UK regulator says disclaimers cannot rescue "typical income" claims. Our ads promise deliverables, not revenue.
- **LinkedIn:** no account sharing, no engagement pods, no comments posted by tools. Ads may not refer to LinkedIn beyond "find me on LinkedIn".
- **AI content:** Meta labels it; TikTok reportedly requires disclosure in ads since July 2026; YouTube requires disclosure of realistic synthetic content.
- **Music:** business accounts are limited to each platform's commercial library.
- **YouTube links:** three link-policy strikes in 90 days can end a channel.

## 10. India

- GST of 18% applies to LinkedIn and Google ads. Enter the GST number on the ad account.
- The 6% equalisation levy on foreign online ads ended in April 2025.
- Recurring card charges above ₹15,000 need extra authentication.
- Targeting foreign founders costs foreign prices. Cheap Indian views are the wrong audience for a worldwide offer.
- Google's new-advertiser credit needs an India billing address and must be applied within 14 days of the first impression. The amount could not be confirmed.
