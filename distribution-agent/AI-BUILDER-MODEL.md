# Model: premium distribution for founders who build with AI

Written 18 November 2026. A service and product model for people who made a product with AI tools and do not know how distribution works. It is the main line of the business; `STRATEGY.md` is the top-level summary.

## How far to trust this

- Nothing here is tested. Every price is a starting point.
- No research was done on what this segment will pay. The premium case rests on judgement and on prices of comparable human services.
- The Lovable figures (about 80% of users want to make money, about 35% do) come from a secondary report of a Wired article.
- The places listed for finding these founders need checking by hand.
- Competitor details come from `COMPETITOR-LANDSCAPE.md` and carry its limits.

## 2. Who we target

Not every AI builder can pay a premium. The premium buyer is a professional with domain knowledge and an income who built a tool for their own industry.

| | Premium target | Not a premium target |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Consultant, agency owner, clinician, broker, coach, operations lead, small-business owner | Hobbyist or student with no income |
| Built with | Lovable, Bolt, Replit, v0, Cursor, Claude Code | Same |
| Product | Solves a problem in their own trade | A general consumer app or a clone |
| Asset they already have | A network in that trade | None |
| What a customer is worth | $300 a year or more | Under $100 |
| Their gap | No idea how buyers find and pay for software | Same |

Qualifying questions before any paid step:

1. Do you earn a living in the industry this product serves?
2. Does a customer pay at least $300 a year?
3. Can a stranger sign up, pay and get a useful result today?
4. Can you give 45 minutes a day for 45 days?

Founders who fail question 2 are offered the 14-day sprint in `STRATEGY.md` instead.

## 3. Why this segment will pay more

- They already pay for building tools and have put in weeks of work.
- They have income from a job or business.
- They want the work done, not a course.
- They hold the best free channel and do not use it. Personal networks produced the first ten customers for 12 of 23 fast-growing business software companies.
- One customer repays a large part of the fee.

## 4. What each step includes

### Free report

As in `STRATEGY.md`: the one customer, 10–20 live posts, the one channel, an opening line per post.

### Distribution Audit: $300

Delivered in five working days.

| Part | Content |
|---|---|
| Demand verdict | Go, change, or stop, with the evidence |
| Positioning | One buyer, one problem, one sentence |
| Readiness check | The six items in section 6, each marked pass or fix |

| Price check | Whether the price fits what a customer is worth |

A "stop" verdict ends the engagement. The founder keeps the audit.

### Launch Programme: $1,500

Each stage is paid on delivery of the stage before. The founder can stop at any stage.

| Stage | Days | We deliver | Founder does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation, $500 | 1–10 | Offer page rewritten; price and trial set; readiness fix list; tracking; script for a 90-second demo | Record the demo; approve the page |
| 2. Reach, $500 | 11–25 | Network map: 50 named people from the founder's own contacts and trade, each with a message. 60 public conversations with opening lines. Two store or directory listings | Send messages, about 45 minutes a day |
| 3. Convert, $500 | 26–45 | Daily reply coaching; paid pilot offer; weekly call; interviews with people who did not buy; scorecard and 90-day plan | Take calls; ask for payment |

Refund for any stage whose deliverables are missed. No promise of revenue.

## 6. Readiness check

AI-built products often fail before distribution starts. We check six things and list fixes. We do not fix code.

| Item | Pass means |
|---|---|
| Payment | A stranger can pay and get access without help |
| First result | A new user reaches something useful in under five minutes |
| Trial or demo | The product can be tried before paying |
| Tracking | Signups and sales can be traced to a source |
| Identity | A named founder, a contact address, terms and privacy pages |
| Stability | The main flow works three times in a row |

This is not a security review. We say so in writing and suggest an independent one before taking customers' data.

## 7. How the method differs for this segment

| Indie hacker | Domain expert who built with AI |
|---|---|
| No network; start with public conversations | Start with their own network, then public conversations |
| Communities such as Reddit and Hacker News | LinkedIn, trade associations, industry groups, past clients |
| Wants coaching | Wants it done |
| Low price, many customers | Higher price, few customers |
| Listing in a developer marketplace | Listing in the trade's own software directories |

## 13. Risks

| Risk | Response |
|---|---|
| Many AI-built products have no demand | The paid audit earns even on a "stop" verdict |
| The product is broken or insecure | Readiness check; written note that we do not review security |
| Premium price with no proof | Three founding clients at half price |
| The segment will not pay | Test with 50 reports before building more |
| Building platforms add marketing features | Stay on the human work they will not do: networks, calls, interviews |
| Human hours | Cap programmes a month; the tool shortens each report |
| Payment from foreign clients | Still the open item in `STRATEGY.md` |
