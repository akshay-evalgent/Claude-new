In 2007, this man lay down on the subway tracks, and let a train run over him. On purpose.
Wesley Autrey, a fifty-year-old construction worker, was waiting for the train in Harlem with his two young daughters.
Then, a nineteen-year-old student on the platform began having a seizure.
He stumbled to the edge, and fell onto the tracks.
And in the dark of the tunnel, the lights of a train appeared.
Wesley left his daughters with a woman nearby, and jumped down.
There was no time to lift him out. So he pushed the young man into the shallow trough between the rails, and lay on top of him.
The driver hit the brakes. But the train couldn't stop in time.
Car after car rolled over them, with only inches to spare. Close enough to leave grease on Wesley's hat.
When the train finally stopped, both men were alive.
People called him the Hero of Harlem. He said somebody had to save that young man, and he was just the closest one.
