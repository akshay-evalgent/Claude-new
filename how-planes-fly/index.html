How planes flyA 20-second look at the four forces of flight

01 · The four forces

Every aircraft in flight is pulled four ways: lift, weight, thrust and drag.

02 · Thrust

The engines throw a huge mass of air backward. That air pushes the plane forward.

03 · Lift

The wing is curved and tilted, so it turns the passing air downward. Pressure drops above the wing and builds below it, pushing the wing up.

04 · Balance

In level cruise the forces cancel out: lift equals weight, and thrust equals drag.

04 · Balance

Add thrust and the plane speeds up. The wing now makes more lift than the plane weighs, and it climbs.
