## 1. The formula in numbers

| Measure | Value |
|---|---|

| Image crossfade | 0.52-0.58 s |
| Dips to black | 5 in 8 minutes (0:16, 2:43, 3:51, 4:58, 6:16), ~0.27 s out, 0.25-0.5 s black, ~0.27 s in |
| Sentences with a visual change within ~1 s of their first word | 87 % (median: 0.15 s after it) |
| New elements per sentence | about 2 |

What it adds up to: **the picture changes slowly, the screen never stops changing.** One drawing holds
for 5-15 s while text lines, arrows, timers and highlights build on top of it, one or two per sentence,
landing on (never after) the words they illustrate.

## 3. Script

- 138 sentences; median 9 words / 3.0 s; 30 % are six words or fewer; 8 % are 20+.
- 30 sentences carry a number; 5 studies are cited on screen (Kubo 2001 twice, Arampatzis 2007, Bohm 2015, Moritani & deVries 1979).
- 4.7 "you/your" per 100 words; only 5 spoken questions (the on-screen text turns many statements into question/answer pairs, e.g. "45 s easy?" / "+ a heavy BACKPACK").
- Repeating moves: question → claim → promise in the first 10 s; one study + one metaphor as proof; "do it right now, start the timer"; open loops ("at the end I'll tell you what this means", "here's where almost everyone messes it up"); every dose has a step up and a step down; asks framed as helping someone else.
- Short lines carry the punch and land on an image change; long sentences are how-to steps held on one locked frame while text builds.

## 6. Text and colour

- Montserrat Bold/ExtraBold. Body lines lower case with one word in caps; titles and labels all caps.
- White = the statement/question. Orange = the answer, the instruction, the character. Green = the dose, the payoff. Red = titles, tendons, warnings. Small grey = asides, units, citations. Amber = safety only.
- Measured: orange on screen in 74 % of frames, red 32 %, green 27 %, blue/purple never. Colour still covers under 1 % of the picture on average.

## 7. Sound

- −14.4 LUFS integrated, −0.8 dBFS peak, 3.1 LU loudness range: heavily compressed, very even voice, mastered to YouTube's level.
- Never silent: a bed sits about 12-15 dB under the voice the whole time (most likely music).
- Pauses trimmed: 44 s of gap in 8 minutes, median 0.13 s.

## 8. What the kit now does to match

- Montserrat replaces Poppins.
- Scenes crossfade in 0.55 s by default (additive, so it is a true dissolve on black); `transition: 'dip'` gives the measured section break; `'cut'` is there too.
- Text lines, text and counters pop in over 80 ms with no slide (`enter: 'rise'` keeps the old style).
- Camera is locked by default; `cam: { push: 1.2-1.8 }` for story shots.
- Figures fade in over 0.55 s by default (`style: 'ink'` keeps the ink-wipe).
- New: `s.tag()` series tag and `s.timer()` running timer that can move to a corner and keep counting.

Not built yet, and the next components worth adding: mocked comment + reply card, LIKE/SUBSCRIBE button
clicks, tier table with slide-in rows, horizontal stat bars, week timeline, tile grid, straight force arrows,
anatomy inset frame, amber warning list.
