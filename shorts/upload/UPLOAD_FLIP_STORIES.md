# Upload kit: flip stories

No episode numbers. Every story stands alone.

**What the format is:** a printed sheet on a table that flips page by page. Each flip shows the same people a few years later.
The reference channels are BGM-Art (created Aug 2026, typical Short 3.6M views) and Folded Moments (created Nov 2, 2026, 8 Shorts over 1M already).
Ours is fully animated (three.js), with our own characters and music.

---

## "Mom is in none of our family photos"

**File:** `flip_mom_photos.mp4` (21.7 s) · **Cover:** `flip_mom_photos_cover.jpg` (the 2026 page)

**Story:** 1999 baby on a blanket → 2005 first day of school → 2010 birthday cake → 2017 graduation → 2023 her wedding.
Mum is always on the right, hidden behind the camera, and every time a page lands the camera flashes.
On the last page, 2026, the daughter holds the camera and mum finally poses: *"my turn, Mom."*
The headline then changes to *"Take a photo of your mom today."*

**Title:**
