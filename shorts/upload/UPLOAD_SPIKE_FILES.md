### Real moving clips for this video

The video currently uses the real frames YouTube serves for each Short (four per Short, cut fast) and captures of the channel's page. To swap in real moving clips, download these four Shorts and put them in a Drive folder called `Spike raw`:

```bash
yt-dlp -f "bv*[ext=mp4][height<=1920]+ba[ext=m4a]/b[ext=mp4]/b" --merge-output-format mp4 -o "%(id)s.%(ext)s" https://www.youtube.com/shorts/y9K86w95L90 https://www.youtube.com/shorts/fnAHLln3MVA https://www.youtube.com/shorts/h2TdBZ2oFPw https://www.youtube.com/shorts/CEjzCx2Z-8Y
```

Which Short is which:
- `y9K86w95L90`: the 31M hit (#162, the stuck head)
- `fnAHLln3MVA`: the "before" (#159, Building LEGO Greedo)
- `h2TdBZ2oFPw`: the hole that does nothing (53M)
- `CEjzCx2Z-8Y`: what fits inside this nose (21M)

Each excerpt is 3 seconds or less, always with our analysis on screen and the channel credited.

## Shano Ghibli and OddVibe

The first versions of these two were made in the earlier, plainer card style. They'll be remade in the new style (real frames, page captures, 3D chart) once the Fusion Bricks look is approved. Their numbers and lessons:

- **Shano Ghibli** (@ShanoGhibli): "Food got 7K views. Feelings got 16 million." Titles about family went 30% → 92%. Lesson: people don't share food, they share grandma.
- **OddVibe** (@OddVibe1): "Letters as a mustache: 15K. Letters to hide a body: 2.6M." Four rankings flopped, the fifth hit. Lesson: don't switch ideas, switch the premise.
