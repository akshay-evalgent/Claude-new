## Titanic: the calm man with the flask

**File:** `titanic_baker.mp4` · **Cover:** `titanic_baker_cover.jpg`

**The detail:** at the stern, right next to Jack and Rose, a man in a white jacket calmly drinks from a flask.
He was real: **Charles Joughin, the Titanic's chief baker**. The video covers what he did that night:
- He sent his 13 bakers up to the lifeboats with bread.
- He refused his own lifeboat command and helped women and children into it.
- He had half a glass of liqueur in his cabin.
- He threw about 50 deck chairs into the sea for people to float on.
- He rode the stern down "like an elevator" and was the last survivor to leave the ship.
- He claimed two hours in the freezing water, and said he hardly felt the cold.
- He was saved at dawn.

Jack's own line from the film ("water that cold… hits you like a thousand knives…") sets up the contrast.

## Jurassic Park: the glass of water

**File:** `jurassic_water_cup.mp4` · **Cover:** `jurassic_water_cup_cover.jpg`

**The detail:** the most famous warning in the film, the rippling glass of water, was one of its hardest shots.
- **The idea** came from Spielberg, whose car mirror was shaking to Earth, Wind & Fire.
- **The mirror** was easy: a little vibrating motor.
- **The water** beat everyone, until Michael Lantieri (Special Dinosaur Effects) found the right note on a guitar one night.
- **On set,** guitar strings ran through the car, and a crew member lay on the floor plucking them on cue.
- **Bonus:** the giant T-rex wasn't waterproof. It soaked up the fake rain, got too heavy and shook on its own, so the crew dried it with blow dryers between takes.

Lantieri tells the key part himself, from Universal's own featurette. The reveal comes at the very end and loops back to the cup.

## Back to the Future: Twin Pines becomes Lone Pine

**File:** `bttf_lone_pine.mp4` (54.8 s) · **Cover:** `bttf_lone_pine_cover.jpg` (the LONE PINE MALL sign)

**The detail:**
1. At the start of the film the mall is **Twin Pines Mall**.
2. Doc drops a clue there: "Old man Peabody owned all of this. He had this crazy idea about breeding pine trees."
3. In 1955 Marty crashes into Peabody's barn. On his way out he smashes through one of Peabody's two pine trees ("You killed a pine!").
4. When Marty gets back to 1985, the sign says **Lone Pine Mall**.

**More of the film this time:** about 44 shots, almost all from the film, and 7 real lines from the film:
- Doc's two Peabody lines;
- "Looks like an airplane." / "That ain't no airplane.";
- "You killed a pine!";
- "You're George McFly." / "Yeah, who are you?"

**Built to be rewatched:**
- **Frame one is already the answer.** The video opens on the LONE PINE MALL sign. The viewer only learns why it matters at the reveal, so many will replay it to look again.
- **The last shot loops straight back.** It is Marty in that same parking lot, so the cut back to the sign looks like the next shot of the scene, not a restart.
- **No outro or call to action.** The last line ends on a question, and the loop starts.

**Built for comments:** it ends on the most argued-about question in the film. Marty spent a week with his teenage parents, so why don't they notice their son looks exactly like "that boy"?
- It quotes the writer's answer ("they only knew him for about a week") and then asks "Do you buy that?"
- That gives two sides to argue, and a yes/no answer that is easy to type.

## Avatar: the floating mountains are real

**File:** `avatar_pandora_real.mp4` (85 s) · **Cover:** `avatar_pandora_real_cover.jpg` (Pandora's floating mountains)

**Layout:** the picture fills the middle 66% of the screen. Blurred bands sit above and below, and the captions are in the bottom band. The thumbnail uses the same layout: "THESE MOUNTAINS" at the top, "ARE REAL" at the bottom.

**The detail:** Pandora's floating "Hallelujah Mountains" are modelled on real sandstone pillars in Zhangjiajie, China. It follows the Titanic pattern: something on screen that everyone assumes is made up turns out to be real.

**Story beats:**
1. **Avatar blues:** fans got so sad Pandora wasn't real that CNN reported on it, and one forum thread about coping passed 1,000 posts.
2. **The reveal:** about 3,000 sandstone pillars, many over 200 m tall, that look like they float in the mist.
3. **The photographer:** the city says a Hollywood photographer's 2008 photos became the model.
4. **The rename:** in January 2010 one pillar was renamed "Avatar Hallelujah Mountain".
5. **The backlash:** about 90% of 60,000 people in an online poll voted against it. The city backed down, but the name stuck.
6. **The elevator:** the 326 m Bailong Elevator climbs the cliff.
7. **Doubt beat:** some say Huangshan inspired it more.
8. **Ending:** "you gotta go where the banshees are… they're waiting for you in China."

**Film lines used:**
- "You are not in Kansas anymore. You're on Pandora." (trailer)
- Jake: "you gotta go where the banshees are."

## Jaws: Quint's USS Indianapolis story is real

**File:** `jaws_indianapolis.mp4` (about 111 s) · **Cover:** `jaws_indianapolis_cover.jpg` (Quint, with "HIS STORY / WAS REAL")

**Layout:** the picture fills the middle 66%, with captions in the bottom band.

**The detail:** Quint's famous late-night speech describes a real 1945 disaster, and the true story goes further than the speech.

**Story beats:**
1. **The ship:** the USS Indianapolis secretly carried parts of the Hiroshima bomb.
2. **The sinking:** four days later two torpedoes sank it in 12 minutes. About 900 men went into the water.
3. **No rescue:** the Navy didn't notice the ship was missing. The men spent four days without food or water, and then the sharks came.
4. **A real survivor** (National Geographic): "you hear a blood-curdling scream… the shark had taken him under."
5. **Found by accident:** a patrol plane spotted them.
6. **Doubt beat:** Quint gets things wrong. It was about 900 men, not 1,100, and July 30, not June 29. Heat and thirst probably killed more men than the sharks.
7. **The captain:** Charles McVay was court-martialed and carried the blame.
8. **The kid:** an 11-year-old Jaws fan, Hunter Scott, made it his school project and took it to Congress. Congress cleared McVay in 2000.
9. **The wreck:** found in 2017, 18,000 ft down.

**Film lines used:**
- "Japanese submarine slammed two torpedoes into our side, Chief."
- "…delivered the bomb. The Hiroshima bomb."
- "The thing about a shark, he's got lifeless eyes… like a doll's eyes."
- "Eleven hundred men went into the water, 316 men come out, the sharks took the rest. June the 29th, 1945."

**Accuracy notes** (in case a commenter asks):
