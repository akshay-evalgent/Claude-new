```
IOPaint is a free, open-source AI that erases people, objects, scratches and text from photos, and it runs on your own laptop (CPU, GPU or Apple Silicon).

Examples: github.com/Sanster/IOPaint (Apache-2.0)
Use it on your own photos. Don't use it to remove watermarks or to fake evidence.
```

## 8. 1969 Moon Radio, Subtitled by AI 🌕

```
1969 Moon Radio, Subtitled by AI 🌕
```
Alternates: `"The Eagle Has Landed", Auto-Subtitled` · `Free Offline Subtitles for Any Video`

**Description:**
```
75 seconds of crackly Apollo 11 radio, transcribed in 22 seconds (after a 9-second model load), offline, on an ordinary 4-core CPU with no GPU (faster-whisper, small.en model, int8). The subtitles are the model's raw output, mistakes included.

How:
pip install faster-whisper

from faster_whisper import WhisperModel
m = WhisperModel("small.en", compute_type="int8")
segs, info = m.transcribe("video.mp4")
for s in segs: print(s.start, s.end, s.text)

Code (MIT): github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper
Footage and audio: NASA (public domain).
```
