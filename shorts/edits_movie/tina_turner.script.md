# Tina Turner: she walked out with 36 cents (draft script)

**Tone:**
- **Abuse:** described non-graphically; ad-safe.
- **Left out:** the 1968 suicide attempt, injury details, her son, and the slur anecdote.
- **The 36-cents line:** must come from Tina herself (CBS / Gayle King), never Angela Bassett in the 1993 film.

## Script (about 100 s; REAL = Tina on camera)

1. **Hook:** In July 1976, Tina Turner ran across a freeway in Dallas with thirty-six cents in her pocket. Eight years later, she was the biggest star in the world.
2. She grew up in Nutbush, Tennessee, as Anna Mae Bullock. As a teenager, she started singing with Ike Turner's band.
3. He gave her a new name, Tina, without asking. She later said it was so he could own her.
4. They married, and the hits came: River Deep, Mountain High. Proud Mary, which won a Grammy.
5. But behind the stage, she later said, Ike controlled everything, and hurt her for years.
6. Then, on the way to a hotel in Dallas, after another fight, she waited until he fell asleep.
7. **REAL (Jonathan Ross, 2017):** "...went out the kitchen way and down to the freeway... I was running across the freeway and this big truck was coming."
8. She reached a Ramada Inn across the freeway. The manager gave her a room.
9. **REAL (CBS):** "I had nothing. I had absolutely nothing. Thirty-six cents, that was all."
10. In the divorce, she gave up the house, the money and the songs. She kept one thing: her name.
11. She paid off the debts from cancelled shows, lived on food stamps, and sang in small clubs. Her solo albums flopped.
12. **Doubt / honesty:** Record labels saw a singer in her forties whose best days were behind her.
13. In 1983, by her account, David Bowie skipped a record-company dinner to see "my favourite singer" in New York. The executives followed him, and saw the crowd.
14. In 1984, at forty-four, she released Private Dancer. "What's Love Got to Do with It" went to number one, her first ever. She was then the oldest woman to top the chart.
15. **REAL (Grammys 1985, her win, or the video).**
16. Four years later in Rio, about a hundred and eighty thousand people came to see her, a Guinness World Record.
17. She found love again, married in Switzerland, and lived until 2023.
18. **Loop:** So thirty-six cents, and a run across a freeway... that's how Tina Turner became Tina Turner.
