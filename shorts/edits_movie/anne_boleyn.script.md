# Anne Boleyn: he broke a church for her, then he killed her (draft script)

**Tone:** dramatic but respectful, with no gore.
- **Visuals:** mostly public-domain portraits, the Vatican letter scans, and real Tower and Hever footage (Historic Royal Palaces, Hever Castle).
- **Dramatised clips:** shown only as brief mood shots, never as evidence.

## Script (about 100 s)

1. **Hook:** A king was so obsessed with this woman that he broke away from the Pope to marry her. Three years later, he had her beheaded.
2. In the 1520s, Henry the Eighth fell for Anne Boleyn. Seventeen of his love letters to her still survive, in the Vatican Library.
3. "My heart and I surrender ourselves into your hands." "I would you were in mine arms, or I in yours."
4. He even signed one with her initials drawn inside a heart.
5. But Anne refused to become just his mistress, as her own sister had been.
6. Henry was already married, to Catherine of Aragon. For six years he begged the Pope for an annulment. The Pope refused.
7. So Henry broke England away from the Catholic Church, made himself its head, and married Anne in secret in 1533.
8. That September, she gave birth: not to the son he wanted, but to a girl, Elizabeth.
9. Then she lost a baby boy, on the very day Catherine was buried.
10. Within months, the king's eye was on another woman: Jane Seymour.
11. On the second of May, 1536, Anne was arrested. The charges: adultery with five men, including her own brother, and plotting the king's death.
12. **Doubt:** Most historians believe the charges were false. Even the Archbishop wrote he "never had better opinion in woman" than in her.
13. The night before, the Tower's constable wrote that she laughed: "I heard say the executioner was very good, and I have a little neck."
14. On the nineteenth of May, she was executed inside the Tower of London, by a swordsman brought from France.
15. That same day, Henry got permission to marry Jane Seymour. They were engaged the next day, and married eleven days later.
16. And the famous portrait of Anne? It was painted decades after she died. No painting from her lifetime survives.
17. But her daughter did. Elizabeth became one of England's greatest queens, and reigned for forty-four years.
18. **Loop:** So the king who broke a church to marry her... was outlived by the daughter he never wanted.
