# Princess Diana: betrayed twice (draft script)

**Tone:** respectful, with no tabloid framing.
- **Interview footage:** the BBC no longer licenses the 1995 Panorama interview, and Prince William asked that it never be aired again. So Diana's lines appear only where news coverage (Channel 4, Sky, ITV, ABC) already includes them, or as on-screen text over a licensed photo. No raw Panorama re-uploads.
- **Wording:** "the inquiry found" / "used deceit". Never "crime" or "forged".
- **Fairness:** the doubt beat includes Dyson's finding that she would probably have agreed anyway, and her own note.
- **Her death:** stated only, with no cause or link to the interview.

## Script (about 100 s)

1. **Hook:** In 1995, Princess Diana told the world her marriage had been betrayed. Twenty-six years later, we found out she had been betrayed again, by the people who filmed her saying it.
2. She married Prince Charles in 1981, at twenty. An estimated seven hundred and fifty million people watched.
3. But the marriage fell apart. In 1994, Charles admitted on TV that he had been unfaithful, once the marriage had, in his words, "irretrievably broken down."
4. A year later, Diana gave her answer, in a secret BBC interview: nearly twenty-three million people watched.
5. **DIANA (via news clip, or text on screen):** "Well, there were three of us in this marriage, so it was a bit crowded."
6. It became one of the most famous interviews ever. The reporter, Martin Bashir, became a star.
7. But in 2020, questions surfaced about how he got it.
8. In 2021, an official inquiry found that Bashir had commissioned fake bank statements, suggesting people close to Diana were being paid to spy on her. He showed them to her brother, and that led to his meeting with Diana.
9. The inquiry called it "deceitful behaviour". Worse, it found the BBC's own investigation in 1996 was "woefully ineffective", and the truth stayed buried for twenty-five years.
10. **REAL (Prince William, 2021):** "...contributed significantly to her fear, paranoia and isolation that I remember from those final years with her."
11. **Doubt:** To be fair, the inquiry also said Diana would probably have agreed to an interview anyway. And in a note weeks later, she wrote she had "no regrets". Bashir said the documents had no bearing on her decision.
12. The BBC apologised, gave money from the interview to charity, and promised never to show it again.
13. Diana never knew. She died in Paris in 1997, two years after the interview.
14. **Loop:** So the most famous interview she ever gave, a story about betrayal, was itself built on a betrayal.
