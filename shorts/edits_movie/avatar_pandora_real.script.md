## Script (about 100 s; film lines marked FILM)

1. **Hook:** If you pay attention to Avatar, you'll notice one thing on Pandora that isn't made up at all.
2. These floating mountains.
3. When Avatar came out, people fell so hard for Pandora that some of them got genuinely sad it wasn't real.
4. CNN even ran a story about it, called "Avatar blues". On one fan forum, a single thread about coping with it got more than a thousand posts.
5. **FILM:** Jake: "Everything is backwards now. Like out there is the true world, and in here is the dream." (if it's in the clips)
6. But those mountains are real.
7. In Zhangjiajie, in China's Hunan province, about three thousand sandstone pillars rise straight out of the forest. Many are over two hundred meters tall.
8. When the mist rolls in, their bases disappear, and they look like they're floating.
9. The city says a Hollywood photographer shot these pillars in 2008, and his photos became the model for Pandora's floating mountains.
10. So in January 2010, just weeks after the movie came out, they renamed one of the pillars "Avatar Hallelujah Mountain".
11. A lot of people in China hated it. In one online poll of sixty thousand people, about ninety percent voted against it.
12. The city backed down. But the name stuck.
13. And to get up there, you can ride the Bailong Elevator, built into the side of a cliff. It climbs three hundred twenty-six meters, the tallest outdoor elevator in the world.
14. **Doubt:** Some say Cameron's team took even more from Huangshan, another mountain range in China. Either way, Pandora was hiding on Earth.
15. **Payoff and loop:** So next time you watch Avatar, look at the floating mountains. They're waiting for you in China.
