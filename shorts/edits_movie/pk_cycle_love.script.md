# He cycled from India to Sweden for love (true story, draft script)

**Format:** a true-story Short with the movie-detail engine: picture in the middle 66%, captions in the bottom band, real interview audio cut in.
**Tone:** warm and romantic, with an underdog. Use "Dalit, once called 'untouchable'", as the BBC and PK himself do. The 2025 documentary *The Cycle of Love* uses re-enacted 1977 scenes; show those only as the film, never as real archive footage.

## Script (about 95 s; REAL marks their own voices, used if found on tape)

1. **Hook:** In 1977, a poor artist from India got on a second-hand bicycle... and rode it all the way to Sweden. For one woman.
2. His name is P.K. Mahanandia. He was born in a village in Odisha, into a Dalit family, once called "untouchable". At school, he was made to sit outside the classroom.
3. But when he was born, his family was told a prophecy: he would marry a woman from a faraway land. She'd be musical, born under Taurus... and she'd own a jungle.
4. Years later, he was drawing tourists in Delhi. A portrait in ten minutes, for ten rupees.
5. One December evening in 1975, a young Swedish woman asked for a portrait. Her name was Charlotte. She had driven all the way to India in a van.
6. She played music. Her family owned a forest. And she was a Taurus.
7. **REAL (PK):** "It was love at first sight." (or his nearest line on tape)
8. Within weeks, they married in his village, in a tribal ceremony. Then she had to go home.
9. For over a year, they wrote letters. He had no money for a plane ticket.
10. So he sold everything he owned, and bought a second-hand bicycle.
11. On the twenty-second of January, 1977, he started pedalling west: Pakistan, Afghanistan, Iran, Turkey.
12. He paid for food by drawing portraits. When his bike broke down in the Afghan desert, a stranger helped him fix it. He slept under the stars. His legs hurt every single day.
13. **Doubt:** He didn't pedal every kilometre. Truck drivers gave him lifts, and a stranger bought him a train ticket. But most of those thousands of kilometres, he cycled.
14. About four months later, in late May, he finally reached Sweden.
15. **REAL:** "When she saw me, she came dashing towards me." (or Lotta telling it)
16. Her family needed some convincing. But they married again, officially, in Sweden.
17. Nearly fifty years later, they're still together, with two children. In 2025, their story became a film.
18. **REAL (PK):** "I was cycling for love, but never loved cycling."
19. **Loop:** So the boy who had to sit outside the classroom... cycled across a continent, for the woman from the prophecy.
