## Why this one is a test

This is the first neon-format episode. Post it in the same daily slot as EP01–03, so the only thing that changes is the **style**. At 48 hours, compare its viewed-vs-swiped and average % viewed against EP01–03 (3D):

- **Neon wins on "viewed vs swiped":** the minimal look stops the scroll better. Make EP05 neon too.
- **Neon wins only on retention:** the story did the work. Keep 3D and port the "callback at 30" structure.
- **Neon loses on both:** stay 3D.
