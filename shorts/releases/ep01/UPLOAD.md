## Upload settings

1. **Upload from the YouTube mobile app** (+ → Short → upload the MP4). Shorts covers can only be set there: tap **Cover**, then pick the frame at about 2.6s or add `cover-3d.png`.
2. **Audience:** **No, it's not made for kids.** It's about parenting and made for adults. "Made for kids" turns off comments and cuts recommendations.
3. **Altered or synthetic content:** **No.** The disclosure is for realistic footage of real people, places or events. This is obviously stylised animation.
4. **Category:** Film & Animation. **Language:** English. **Caption certification:** none needed, there's no dialogue audio.
5. **Comments:** On. Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review. **Remixing:** allow, because remixes and duets spread it.
6. **Visibility:** Public immediately. Don't schedule your first Shorts, and never delete and re-upload one, because a re-upload loses its initial test audience.

## When to post

Parents scroll after kids' bedtime. Aim for **7–9 pm in the audience's time zone**; for the US that's about 7:30 pm Eastern. Weekend mornings are a strong second slot. Then hold the same time daily so results are comparable.
