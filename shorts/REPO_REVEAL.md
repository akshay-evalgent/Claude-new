# Repo Reveal: real demo clips, with a tease, hook, retention and payoff

Every Short shows a **real result from a free tool**, then gives the method so the viewer can do it themselves. It's built with `fx_clips.py` (picture-in-picture, before/after wipes, zooms, flipbooks), `cards.py` (how-to cards) and `breakdown.py` (assembly, captions, narration and music).

## The script formula (20–30 seconds)

| Beat | Time | Job | Rule | Example (cat video) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Tease** | 0–3s | Show a glimpse of the payoff with no explanation | The most surprising frame first. One short line that makes people ask "how?" | *"This cat is copying her face."* |
| **2. Hook** | 3–8s | Say what it is and why it matters to *them* | Free, on GitHub, does X with Y. Name the surprising constraint ("one still photo"). | *"Every one of these is a still photo. A free AI on GitHub makes them move."* |
| **3. Retention** | 8–15s | Escalate, and open a loop | 2–3 quick examples, each wilder than the last. **Promise something at the end**: "wait for the panda", "the last one is unreal". | paintings, then cartoons, then *"…wait for the panda"* |
| **4. Method** | 15–21s | How to do it | One card with the exact steps, link or command, so people screenshot it and save the video | the LivePortrait steps card |
| **5. Payoff** | 21–26s | Deliver the promised moment | Best clip, little or no narration, so the visual lands | the panda |
| **6. CTA** | last 2–3s | Save, send, comment | One action, tied to a person: "send it to someone with a pet", "comment your city" | *"Save this. Send it to someone with a pet."* |

**Why it gets replays and shares:**
- The tease frame is the most striking image, so people swipe back to see it again.
- The open loop keeps them watching past the middle.
- The method card makes them save it.
- The CTA names a specific friend to send it to.

**Honesty rules:**
- Only show **real outputs**: the project's own demos, or results we produced.
- State any limits on screen. For example, LivePortrait's animal mode needs an NVIDIA GPU. CodeFormer's colour and scratch repair run locally, and the online demo only does restoration.
- AI restoration **reconstructs** detail. Say so in the description: it's great for memories, not for evidence.
