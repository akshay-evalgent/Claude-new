# Evidence: dolphin calf freed (Lemon Bay, Florida, March 2019)

## Every on-screen fact
| On screen | Source |
|---|---|
| “BABY DOLPHIN”, “FISHING LINE” CUT INTO ITS TAIL | NOAA article (reposted by [Mote Marine Laboratory, Mar 12 2019](https://mote.org/?p=2698)): "fishing line cutting through each of side of the the calf's tail"; line "going through the calf's mouth, forming a 'bridle'" |
| SEEN FOR WEEKS, TANGLED IN LINE | same: "reported over the past few weeks in Lemon Bay, near Englewood, Florida" with "fishing line and gear wrapped around its body and tail, and through its mouth" |
| A TEAM FROM 9 GROUPS CAME TO HELP | same: "biologists and veterinarians from nine organizations involved in stranding response", led by the Chicago Zoological Society's Sarasota Dolphin Research Program |
| THEY CAUGHT MOM AND BABY, TO KEEP THEM CALM | same: "mission to catch both mom and her calf"; "to keep mom and her calf calm, biologists held both close together"; NOAA video page: "captured together to minimize stress on the calf" |
| THEN THEY PULLED THE LINE OUT | NOAA video (subtitle): "The team removed the line and released the gear" |
| “FINALLY” THE CALF WAS FREE | article: "After about an hour of treatment the calf was free of fishing line and gear" |
| “BOTH SWAM AWAY” | article: "both animals swam away looking strong" |

Extra (description only): "The pair exchanged whistles with each other as they were held." Rescue date: March 11, 2019 (article of March 12 says "yesterday").
