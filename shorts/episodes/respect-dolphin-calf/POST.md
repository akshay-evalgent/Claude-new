## Description
```
For weeks, a baby dolphin swam through Lemon Bay, Florida with fishing line cutting into its tail and running through its mouth. In March 2019, a team from nine organizations caught the calf and its mom together to keep them calm, cut the line away, and let them go. Mom and baby whistled to each other the whole time. 🐬❤️‍🩹

Respect to the rescuers.
```
