## Post 10 · Debate (text + debate card variant)
Per-seat pricing is a tax on growth.
Hire one person, pay five vendors.

A 10-person team on five per-seat tools at $25 a seat pays $1,250 a month.
At 20 people, $2,500. The tools did not get better. The team got bigger.

Flat-rate and open-source tools price the job, not the headcount.

The defence of per-seat: it is simple, it scales down too, and vendors need predictable revenue.

Which side?
A: Per-seat is fair. You pay for who uses it.
B: Per-seat punishes hiring. Price the job.
