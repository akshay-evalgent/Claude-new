# Roadmap: warm up on startups, automations and tools, then build to the Teardown

The Teardown and swap formats need real swaps you have run, with the numbers and the failures. The page should not wait for those. Weeks 1 to 3 warm up with content that needs no claimed experience: curated open-source tools with live star counts, automation recipes, honest comparisons, opinions, and polls that tell us what the audience pays for. Week 4 onwards layers in the Teardown once there is an audience to send bills and at least two swaps you have actually done.

## Phase 1 · Warm-up (weeks 1 to 3)
Content pillars, in this ratio:
- **Tools for startups (40%).** Open-source and low-cost picks by job (CRM, support, analytics, automation, docs). Star counts from the GitHub API, checked the day of posting. What it replaces, where it bites, who should not use it. No prices unless from a screenshot taken that day.
- **Automations (30%).** Node-by-node recipes a startup can build in an afternoon: lead intake, support triage, invoice chasing, weekly numbers, content repurposing. Rendered as flow slides. Instructional, so no "we ran this" claims are needed.
- **Opinions and debates (20%).** Per-seat pricing, hire-vs-automate, self-host-vs-pay, tool sprawl. Stated plainly, two sides given, question at the end.
- **Input (10%).** Polls and question cards: what do you pay, what would you automate first, what tool do you barely open. Every answer feeds Phase 2.

Gates to leave Phase 1: 1,000 followers, 200 newsletter subscribers, at least 20 comments naming a specific tool or bill, and two swaps you have personally completed with notes.

## Phase 2 · Introduce the Teardown (weeks 4 to 6)
- Tuesday becomes Teardown day. First teardown is a bill a reader sent, not yours, so the numbers are theirs and the credit is theirs.
- Swap cards use only swaps you have run. `content/later/TEARDOWN-WEEK-PLAN.md` is the ready week for this; fill its brackets from your notes.
- Friday reader swaps begin as soon as one reader sends one.

## Phase 3 · Monetise (week 7 onwards)
Media kit at 3,000 followers. First sponsor from hosting, open-source cloud tiers or automation platforms. Affiliate links in the newsletter, disclosed, from week 4. Template pack when a recipe post gets 50 or more "how do I build this" comments.

## What stays constant across phases
Voice guide. Two posts a day in the sprint, one a day after. The 90-minute routine. Comment as the page. Weekly honest recap on Saturday. Topic never drifts from startup tools, automations and software cost.
