# Page setup checklist, with your personal profile kept out of sight

## What is public and what is not (verified vs likely)
- **Page admins are not listed publicly.** LinkedIn shows followers a page's posts, About, People (members who list it as employer) and affiliated pages, not its admins. Third-party guides agree; LinkedIn's help centre does not publish a single statement on it, so treat it as "well established" rather than "official".
- **Your name becomes visible only if you** list the page in your Experience, post about it or react to its posts from your profile, or are tagged by someone. Do none of those.
- **"Invite connections to follow" very likely shows your name** to the invitee ("Akshay invited you to follow Lean Stack"). I could not confirm this from an official source, so treat it as unsafe. Either skip invites entirely or give a trusted co-admin the job and let their name carry it.
- **The People tab** shows anyone who lists the page as employer. Keep it empty or let only a co-admin appear.
- **Page comments and posts show the page identity only.** Commenting as the page is anonymous.

## Step by step
1. From your profile: Work (grid icon) → Create a Company Page → Company. You must create from a real profile; this does not expose you.
2. Name: Lean Stack. Public URL: `leanstack` (fallbacks `theleanstack`, `lean-stack-hq`). Website: your Tally or Gumroad page for now. Industry: Technology, Information and Media. Size: 2 to 10. Type: Self-employed or Privately held. Tagline: from `page-copy.md`.
3. Upload `assets/leanstack/logo-400.png` and `assets/leanstack/banner-A-1128x191.png` (if LinkedIn asks for the new wide format, use `banner-A-4200x700.png`). Variant B is the alternative with the mini swap on the right.
4. Paste the About section, specialties and hashtags from `page-copy.md`. Set the custom button to "Learn more" pointing at the Tally teardown-request form until the newsletter exists.
5. Do not add Lean Stack to your own Experience. Do not follow, react to or share the page from your profile. Mute the temptation: the page's growth comes from the plan, not from you.
6. Add one co-admin you trust (Content admin is enough). They can send follow invites if you want that lever at all.
7. Create the Tally form "Send us your software bill" (tool list, seats, monthly cost, must-keep feature, optional email). Put the link in Featured once there is a post to feature.
8. Post the launch carousel and pin it. Post the first comment with links within one minute. Start commenting as the page.
9. At 150 followers: Admin tools → Create newsletter. Name "Lean Stack Weekly". Logo `newsletter-logo-300.png`, cover `newsletter-cover-1920x1080.png`. Publish issue 1 from `newsletter-issue-01.md` with `edition-01-cover-1920x1080.png`. Launch fires the one-time subscribe invite to all followers; best done at 300 to 500 followers.
10. Featured: pin the latest newsletter, the best carousel, and the teardown request form.
11. Analytics check every Sunday (see the playbook). Export follower demographics at day 30 for the media kit.

## Working folder hygiene
- Keep one folder per post: spec JSON, the rendered PDF, pricing-page screenshots with dates, the post text, the first comment text.
- Never post a price without its screenshot in the folder.
