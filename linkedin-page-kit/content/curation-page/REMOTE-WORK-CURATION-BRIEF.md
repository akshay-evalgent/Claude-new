# Remote work for international clients · curation page brief

Prepared 6 Nov 2026. Figures come from platform pages, vendor surveys and news coverage read through search summaries (most primary sites are blocked from this session). Every number is tagged **advertised** (what the platform says), **reported** (what workers or surveys say) or **arithmetic** (my own multiplication from a stated rate). Screenshot the source before any figure goes on a card.

---

## 1 · The page in one line

A page that finds remote work an Indian can do for international clients from home, checks that it is real, open to India and paid in foreign currency, and shows what it actually pays and how the money reaches a bank account.

The gap: LinkedIn is full of "remote AI jobs" posts, but almost none of them say (a) whether India is eligible, (b) the real rate versus the advertised rate, (c) how the person got paid. Those three checks are the product. Everything else is formatting.

Four readers, in order of how much they will pay:

1. **The switcher.** Salaried, 3 to 10 years in, wants a US/EU remote role or contract. Will pay for a filtered tracker.
2. **The side-earner.** Wants 10 to 20 hours a week on an AI training platform or tutoring. Will pay a small amount for "which platforms pay, which don't".
3. **The consultant.** 8 to 15 years in a function, wants retainers from abroad instead of a desk job. Will pay for pricing data and client sources.
4. **The hirer.** Startups and agencies abroad who want vetted Indian talent. Will pay for featured listings and an opt-in list. This is where the real money is.

## 2 · The five lanes

### Lane A · AI training and expert platforms

Who it suits: anyone with a real domain (medicine, law, finance, maths, software) and good written English; also generalists willing to do rubric work.

Talent required
- Domain depth they can prove: degree, licence, or 3+ years in the field. Doctors, lawyers, CAs, PhDs get the top tiers.
- Written English that reads clean. Most tasks are "judge two answers, explain why".
- Coding tracks: Python or JavaScript, reading other people's code, writing tests.
- Patience with onboarding: assessments, AI interviews, waitlists that go quiet for weeks.
- Tools: nothing beyond a browser, a quiet room and a stable connection.

Pay, with the gap that makes the story
| Platform | Advertised | Reported | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mercor | avg ~$85–115/hr; domain experts $50–150; oncologists $350–450; lawyers $100–150 | India-tagged listings median ~$21/hr, max ~$30 | Pays in rupees via Stripe |
| Alignerr | "$80 average" | median ~$22.50/hr | Project-based; gaps between projects |
| Outlier | generalist up to $15/hr; experts up to $90–150/hr; some per-task ~$118 | varies by queue | Work dries up without warning; many India accounts report pauses |
| Turing | up to $50/hr (US-facing) | India median ~₹881/hr (~$10); global median $15–27/hr | Long-term contracts, 160 h/month |
| micro1 | senior engineers $100–150/hr | — | AI interview gate |

Earnings examples (arithmetic from the reported rate, ₹85/$ assumed, check the day's rate)
- Mercor at the India median $21/hr, 20 h/week: ~$1,800/month ≈ ₹1.5L.
- Alignerr at $22.50/hr, 15 h/week: ~$1,460/month ≈ ₹1.2L.
- Outlier generalist $15/hr, 20 h/week: ~$1,300/month ≈ ₹1.1L. Expert queue $90/hr, 10 h/week: ~$3,900/month ≈ ₹3.3L.
- Turing India ₹881/hr, 160 h/month: ≈ ₹1.4L/month.

Story the page tells: "Mercor says $85 an hour. Indian accounts see $21." That card alone is a week of comments.

How they get paid: platform payout (Mercor: Stripe in INR; Outlier: PayPal/AirTM; Turing: bank transfer; Alignerr: check). Nothing to invoice. The page's job here is to say which platforms are paying this month and which have paused.

Where the page curates from: platform careers pages, Reddit and LinkedIn posts by people on the platforms ("my first Mercor payout", "Outlier paused my account"), weekly. Credit and link every post.

### Lane B · AI automation and agent freelancing

Who it suits: people who can build with n8n, Make, Python and LLM APIs and explain it in plain English to a small-business owner abroad.

Talent required
- n8n or Make, plus Python for the bits those cannot do.
- APIs and webhooks; OpenAI or Anthropic APIs; a vector database for RAG; basic evals.
- One vertical you can speak: dental clinics, real-estate agents, recruiting agencies, e-commerce ops. Generalists get $29/hr; vertical specialists get $60+.
- Three case studies with Loom walkthroughs. Nobody abroad hires on a CV for this.
- A fixed-scope offer: "lead-reply bot in 7 days, $1,500", then a monthly retainer.

Earnings examples (arithmetic)
- Upwork median $29.50/hr, 80 h/month, minus 10% Upwork fee: ~$2,100 ≈ ₹1.8L.
- Specialist $60/hr, 80 h/month: ~$4,800 ≈ ₹4.1L before fees.

How they get paid: Upwork direct-to-local-bank (~$0.99 per withdrawal) or Payoneer; direct clients through Skydo, Wise or Payoneer (section 3). Needs invoices, purpose codes, FIRA.

Where the page curates from: Upwork and Contra postings (count, median, top rate each week), LinkedIn posts by Indian builders showing a client result, founders abroad asking "who can build this".

### Lane C · Remote AI roles at startups (contract or full-time)

Who it suits: engineers, PMs, designers, writers, ops people who want a real team abroad while living in India.

Talent required
- Titles that are hiring: AI engineer (LLM apps), ML engineer, forward-deployed engineer, solutions engineer, evals/quality lead, AI product manager, technical writer, annotation/data-ops lead, growth with AI tooling.
- Python, one LLM stack, cloud basics, evals. For non-engineers: shipped work with AI tools, written async communication.
- Four hours of overlap with US Pacific or EU. State it on the profile.
- "Worldwide" or "India OK" eligibility, which is the thing nobody checks. Most "remote" US roles are US-only.

Pay: no single clean source. Use the role's own posted range (many US postings show one) and convert. Tell readers it is the posting's number, not ours. Typical contractor ranges we saw in postings: $3–8K/month for mid-level engineers hired from India, $8–15K for senior. Tag these **reported from postings** and refresh weekly.

How they get paid: contractor agreement, monthly invoice, Deel/Remote/Rippling EOR if the company uses one (they handle FIRA and tax docs), otherwise Skydo/Wise.

Where the page curates from: Remote Rocketship, Remotive, Himalayas, WeWorkRemotely, Wellfound remote filter, YC Work at a Startup, company careers pages, founders posting "we're hiring, remote, worldwide". Manual, 40 roles a week, filtered to India-eligible.

### Lane D · Fractional and consulting for international clients

Who it suits: 8 to 15+ years in finance, marketing, engineering, HR, ops, data; wants two or three clients abroad, not one boss.

Talent required
- A repeatable offer: audit (fixed fee) → 90-day roadmap → monthly retainer at one or two days a week.
- One public case study with numbers the client approved.
- Pricing in day rates or retainers, never hourly.
- A short written point of view people can forward.
- Time zone honesty: Europe is the easy market from India; US means evenings.

Pay (US benchmarks, vendor surveys 2025–26; no India benchmark found, say so)
| Role | Monthly retainer (median) | Hourly |
|---|---|---|
| Fractional exec, all functions | ~$5,750 | — |
| Fractional CMO | ~$8,250 | $200–500 |
| Fractional CTO | ~$8,000 | — |
| Fractional CFO | — | $175–450 |

Indian consultants selling abroad typically land 40 to 60% of those numbers. Tag that as **estimate** and ask readers to correct it in the comments. That correction thread becomes the page's own data.

Earnings examples (arithmetic)
- Two EU clients at $3,000/month, one day a week each: $6,000 ≈ ₹5.1L/month.
- One audit at $2,500 plus one retainer at $4,000: $6,500 in the first month.

How they get paid: invoice in USD/EUR/GBP, Skydo or Wise, LUT for GST, 44ADA for income tax (section 3).

Where the page curates from: LinkedIn posts by Indian fractional execs showing a client (credit them), international "fractional" job boards, founders abroad asking for part-time CFO/CMO help.

### Lane E · Flexible, non-desk, not full-time

Who it suits: people who do not want a desk job or a 40-hour week: parents at home, people between things, students, retirees with good English.

Talent required
- Tutoring: fluent English, neutral accent, a quiet room, headset, 2 to 4 hours in the India evening (matches Gulf, Southeast Asia and early Europe demand). TEFL helps on Preply, not required on Cambly.
- Voice and data work: clear diction, reading scripts, labelling audio or images; no degree.
- Virtual assistant and customer support: email, calendar, CRM, Intercom or Zendesk, 4 hours of client overlap.
- Bookkeeping: QuickBooks or Xero certification; US and UK small businesses outsource this constantly.

## 3 · How the money actually arrives (this is the section nobody else writes)

### Platform payouts
Mercor pays INR via Stripe. Outlier via PayPal or AirTM. Cambly via PayPal. Preply via PayPal, Payoneer or Skrill. Upwork direct to Indian bank (~$0.99) or Payoneer. Turing by bank transfer. Confirm each on the platform's help page before it goes on a card.

### Direct clients: what a $2,000 invoice becomes (researched Nov 2026, verify each on the pricing page)
| Rail | Fee | Receive, roughly |
|---|---|---|
| Skydo | flat $19 up to $2,000; $29 up to $10,000; 0.3% above; no FX markup; free FIRA | ~$1,981 |
| Wise | mid-market plus ~1.6–1.9%; FIRA ~$2–2.5 | ~$1,962–1,968 |
| Payoneer | up to 2% FX plus receiving fees; free digital FIRA | ~$1,955–1,960 |
| PayPal | ~4.4% + fixed, plus ~3% FX | ~$1,855 |
| Razorpay MoneySaver | bank-linked, auto FIRA; check current fee | — |

PayPal versus Skydo on one $2,000 invoice is about $125, or ₹10,000 and change. Twelve invoices a year is a laptop. That is a post.

### Paperwork that trips people
- **FIRA / FIRC.** The certificate that proves the money came from abroad. Needed for GST zero-rating and for peace of mind in a tax notice. Skydo and Payoneer give it free; banks charge.
- **Purpose code.** Every inward remittance needs one (P0802 for software consultancy, P1006 for business and management consultancy, P0807 for other IT services, etc.). Wrong code means calls from the bank.
- **Income tax, Section 44ADA presumptive scheme.** Declare 50% of receipts as profit, no books, if receipts are under ₹50L (₹75L if 95% or more comes digitally, which it does for this crowd). The Income-tax Act 2025 renumbers sections from 1 April 2026; the new number differs across sources (58 versus 194), so quote "presumptive scheme for professionals" and link the official page rather than a number.
- **GST.** Registration is mandatory above ₹20L turnover even if 100% is export. File a Letter of Undertaking (RFD-11) each financial year so exports are zero-rated without paying IGST. GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B still fall due. The invoice must carry "Supply meant for export under LUT without payment of IGST".
- **Contracts.** One page: scope, rate, currency, payment within 15 days, IP transfer on payment, either side can end with 30 days' notice. The page gives this template away free; it is the lead magnet.

## 7 · Rules that keep it clean

- Curate by hand. Never scrape LinkedIn; it is against their terms and the account will go.
- Every repost links to and names the original poster. If they ask, it comes down the same day.
- Never list a role or platform that charges the candidate anything. It is the single biggest scam signal in this niche and the page's reputation is the product.
- Label every figure: advertised, reported, or our arithmetic. Never say "earn ₹X from home". Say "this platform's India listings show a median of $21; here's the maths at 20 hours".
- Check India eligibility on the day of posting and mark closed roles closed.
- Disclose any referral link in the comment where it appears.
- Opt-in only for the talent list; nobody goes on it without filling the form.
- Your personal profile stays out of it, same rules as Founder Signal: page admin only, no invites from your network, no likes from your account.
