# Page copy

Written for the recommended direction, **Lean Stack**. Swap the name and tagline if you pick another option from `BRAND-OPTIONS.md`; the About section and rules hold for all six.

## Name
**Lean Stack**  
Handle to try: `linkedin.com/company/leanstack` (fallbacks: `theleanstack`, `lean-stack-hq`).

Why this name:
- It states the promise in three words. Every viral post on the page is a cost-saved number, and the name is the number.
- "Zero" and "$0" render as a visual mark (the logo) without explanation.
- It is not a trademark of a tool (avoid "GitHub", "OpenAI", "n8n" in the name; GitHub's trademark policy prohibits using its name in product or page names).
- It survives a pivot: tools, repos, workflows, prompts and hiring all fit under "zero cost".

Alternatives considered: The $0 Stack (hard to type and search), Frugal AI (reads cheap, not smart), Open Stack Business (vague), Repo Radar (too developer-only for a business audience).

## Tagline (120 characters max)
Do more with less software. One swap a day: the expensive tool, the free or cheap alternative, the exact saving.

## About section
Most small businesses pay $500 to $3,000 a month for software that open-source projects and free tiers already do.

Lean Stack shows you the swap, every day:
• The open-source repo or free tier that replaces a paid tool, and exactly what it saves
• When free is not good enough, the tool that costs 5 to 10x less and still does the job
• AI workflows you can copy in an afternoon (n8n, local models, cheap API credits)
• Cost teardowns of real business stacks, rebuilt for a tenth of the bill
• Weekly newsletter: the five best swaps, with what you keep and what you give up

No tips you can't act on today. No tool we haven't run ourselves.

Subscribe to the newsletter below, or follow for the daily drop.

## Voice rules
- Lead with the number. Dollars saved, hours saved, stars, seats.
- Write for the owner or ops lead, not the developer. Explain what it replaces before how it works.
- Always include "start in one line" or the free-tier limit so the reader can act today.
- Always include YOU GIVE UP. A swap with no trade-off reads as an ad and gets ignored.
- Never post a tool we have not opened. Never quote a price we have not checked on the vendor's pricing page that week.
- No external links in the post body. Repo link goes in the first comment.
