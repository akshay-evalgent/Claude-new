# Page copy: Founder Signal

## Name
**Founder Signal**  
Handle: `linkedin.com/company/thefoundersignal`.

Why: the page does three things (news, swaps, thought experiments) and the only word that covers all three is "signal". "Founder" names the reader. It does not promise "free" or "cheap", so paid-but-better picks and AI news both fit.

## Tagline (120 characters max)
AI news, tool swaps and thought experiments for people building companies.

## About section
Three things, every day, for people building companies.

What happened in AI and startups today, told straight: who said it, what the number was, why it matters. No hype, sources in the first comment.

The Swap: one paid tool, and the free or open-source way to do the same job. What you keep, what you give up, what it actually costs in time. Written by people who pay for software with their own card.

Thought experiments: the question a founder should sit with this week. If the best AI model scores 12% on expert work today, what does your team look like when it scores 60%?

Weekly newsletter on Saturdays. Reply to anything; we read all of it.
