# Founder Signal · start here

This folder is everything you need to create the LinkedIn page and post for the first week, in the order to do it. You do not need to open the GitHub repo. Work through parts A to D once; after that, posting is 5 minutes per post.

Page name: **Founder Signal**. Handle to request: **thefoundersignal**. Newsletter: **Founder Signal Weekly**.

---

## Part A · Create the page (20 minutes, once)

1. Log in to LinkedIn on a desktop browser with your own account. You need a personal account to create a page; it does not show on the page.
2. Click the **For Business** icon (grid, top right) → **Create a Company Page** → **Company**.
3. Fill the form with the text from `01-page-setup/page-copy.md`:
   - Name: Founder Signal
   - LinkedIn public URL: thefoundersignal (if taken, try founder-signal, then thefoundersignalhq)
   - Website: leave blank for now, or paste your Tally form link if you made one
   - Industry: Technology, Information and Media
   - Company size: 2-10 employees
   - Company type: Self-employed
   - Tagline: paste the tagline line from page-copy.md
4. Logo: upload `01-page-setup/logo-400.png`.
5. Tick the verification box and click **Create page**.
6. You land on the admin view. Click the pencil next to the cover area and upload `01-page-setup/banner-A-1128x191.png`. If LinkedIn says the image is too small, upload `banner-A-4200x700.png` instead.
7. Click **Edit page** → **About** → paste the About section from page-copy.md. Add the Specialties words. Save.
8. **Edit page** → **Buttons**: choose "Learn more" and paste your Tally link, or skip until the newsletter exists.
9. Do not add Founder Signal to your personal profile's Experience, do not use "Invite connections", and do not like or share the page from your personal account. The page stays separate from you.

Optional but recommended: **Settings → Manage admins** → add one trusted person as Content admin so two people can post.

## Part B · Make the Tally form (10 minutes, once)

1. Go to tally.so, create a free account, **New form**.
2. Title: "Send us the tool you resent paying for". Fields: Tool and plan (short text), Seats (number), Monthly cost (short text), The one feature you cannot lose (long text), Email (optional).
3. Publish, copy the link. Put it in the page's website field and keep it for first comments that say [your Tally form link].

## Part C · How to post (read once)

Every post folder in `POSTS/` contains:
- `image.png` (and `image-square.png`) or `carousel.pdf`
- `caption.txt`: the exact text, with hashtags. The first line may say TAG IN THE POST: type @ in the LinkedIn box and pick that company page from the dropdown, where the name appears in the caption.
- `first-comment.txt`: post this as the first comment, then pin it.
- `how-to-post.txt`: the clicks for that post type.

The three post types:

**Image post.** Admin view → Start a post → photo icon → upload image.png → paste caption.txt → Post. Then open the post, comment with first-comment.txt, click the three dots on your comment → Pin.

**Carousel (document) post.** Start a post → the "+" icon → Add a document → upload carousel.pdf → give it the title from the first line of the caption → paste caption.txt → Post. Comment and pin as above.

**Poll.** Start a post → "+" icon → Create a poll → question and options from caption.txt → 3 days → paste the context text into the main box → Post. Comment and pin.

Rules that protect reach:
- Never put a web link in the post text. Links go in the first comment only.
- Post from the page, not your profile. Check the avatar at the top of the post box.
- Where a caption says [today's date] or [N], replace it before posting. Never post a bracket.
- After posting, stay for 90 minutes. Reply to every comment within 15 minutes, 15 or more words, and end with a question back. Never "Thanks for sharing".
- Best times are already in each folder's how-to file. 8:30 am IST reaches India and Europe; 8:30 pm IST reaches the US morning.

## Part E · Newsletter (once the page has 150 followers)

1. Admin view → **Admin tools** (top right) → **Create a newsletter**.
2. Name: Founder Signal Weekly. Description: from `02-newsletter/newsletter-issue-01.md` header. Logo: `02-newsletter/newsletter-logo-300.png`. Cover: `02-newsletter/newsletter-cover-1920x1080.png`.
3. Creating it sends a one-time invitation to every follower. Best done at 300 to 500 followers, but do not wait past week two.
4. Publish issue 1: paste `02-newsletter/newsletter-issue-01.md`, cover `02-newsletter/edition-01-cover-1920x1080.png`. Saturdays, 9 am IST.

## Part F · Every week after this

Each weekday the page needs two news cards and one swap or thought experiment. Send me the story (a link or a screenshot) and any headshot, and I return the folder for that post: image, caption, first comment. Photos of people must come from press kits, Wikimedia Commons or the person's own public profile, and the credit goes in the first comment.
