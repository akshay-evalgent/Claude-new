# Services agreement (one page)

**Between** [Your name or company], [city], India ("Provider")
**and** [Client legal name], [registered address], [country] ("Client")
**Date:** [date]

1. **Work.** Provider will deliver: [three lines: what, by when, what is not included]. Changes to scope are agreed in writing (email is fine) with a price before work starts.
2. **Fee.** [USD X fixed / USD X per day]. [50% on signing, 50% on delivery] / [Invoiced monthly]. Fees exclude Client-side bank or platform charges. Provider is an independent contractor; no taxes are withheld by Client unless law requires it, and Provider's invoices carry any Indian GST treatment that applies.
3. **Payment.** Within 15 days of invoice, by [Skydo / Wise / Payoneer] in [USD]. Late payment pauses work after 7 days' notice.
4. **Ownership.** Client owns the deliverables once paid in full. Provider keeps the right to reuse general know-how, tools and non-client-specific code, and to name Client as a client unless Client says no in writing.
5. **Confidentiality.** Each side keeps the other's non-public information private for 2 years.
6. **Ending it.** Either side may end this agreement with 30 days' written notice. Work done to that date is paid.
7. **Liability.** Each side's liability is capped at the fees paid under this agreement in the previous 3 months. No liability for indirect loss.
8. **Law.** [Client's country] law; disputes first by discussion, then mediation, then courts of [city]. (Or: Indian law, courts of Bengaluru.)

Signed: ______________ (Provider)   ______________ (Client)
