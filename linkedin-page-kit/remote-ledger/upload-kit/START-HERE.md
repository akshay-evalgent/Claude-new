# Remote Ledger · start here

Everything to create the page and post the first week, in order. Work through A to D once. After that each post is five minutes plus the reply window.

Page name: **Remote Ledger**. Handle to request: **remoteledger** (fallbacks: theremoteledger, remote-ledger).

---

## A · Create the page (20 minutes, once)

1. Desktop browser, logged in to your own LinkedIn. For Business (grid icon, top right) → Create a Company Page → Company.
2. Fill from `01-page-setup/page-copy.md`: Name Remote Ledger · URL remoteledger · Website blank for now · Industry Staffing and Recruiting · Size 2–10 · Type Self-employed · Tagline from the file.
3. Logo: `01-page-setup/logo-400.png`. Tick the box, Create page.
4. Cover: pencil on the cover area → `01-page-setup/banner-A-1128x191.png` (if "too small": `banner-A-4200x700.png`).
5. Edit page → About: paste the About and Specialties from page-copy.md. Save.
6. Do not add the page to your Experience, do not invite connections, do not react from your profile. Add one trusted Content admin.

## B · Three Tally forms and the tracker page (40 minutes, once)

Fields for each form are spelled out in `01-page-setup/SETUP-CHECKLIST.md`, step 7. Make Pitch, Request and Waitlist. Put the Pitch link in the page's Website field and the "Learn more" button.

Tracker: Notion (free) → new page "Remote Ledger tracker" → Import → `03-tracker/tracker-2026-11-09.csv` → Share → Publish to web. Copy the public link; it goes in Monday's first comment where it says [paste your Notion tracker link].

Contract template: make a Google Doc or Notion page from `04-templates/contract-one-page.md`, set it to "anyone with the link can view", keep the link for Saturday's first comment.

## C · How to post (read once)

Every folder in `POSTS/` has: `image.png` (and `image-square.png`) or `carousel.pdf` · `caption.txt` · `first-comment.txt` · `how-to-post.txt` with the clicks.

Three post types: image, document (carousel), poll. The clicks are in each folder's how-to file.

Rules that protect reach:
- No web link in the post text, ever. Links go in the first comment, which you pin.
- Post as the page. Check the avatar at the top of the post box.
- Replace every [bracket] before posting. Never post a bracket.
- Stay 90 minutes after posting. Reply within 15 minutes, 15+ words, end with a question back.
- One post a day. Never two within three hours.

## D · The first week

`POSTS/00-POSTING-ORDER.md` has the table. Mon 9 Nov tracker · Tue 10 pay card · Wed 11 the $2,000 invoice · Thu 12 the pitch form opens · Fri 13 not a desk job · Sat 14 the paperwork carousel · Sun 15 the poll.

Sunday evening: followers, impressions per post, comments per post, and the three Tally counts (pitches, requests, waitlist). Send me those four numbers and I build week two.

## E · Every week after

`06-docs/PLAYBOOK.md` is the routine, day by day, with the sources. The short version: send me screenshots or links of public asks from LinkedIn (I can't open LinkedIn from here), the Tally export of pitches, and any reader number you want printed. I return a folder per post.
