# The three Tally forms (free account at tally.so → New form → add fields in this order)

## 1 · Pitch (providers in India)
Title: "Pitch to clients abroad · Remote Ledger"
Intro line: "Free. We check five things and publish the pitches that pass on Thursdays. We never charge to pitch or to apply."
1. What you do, in 12 words or fewer (short text, required)
2. Who it's for: one kind of client (short text, required) e.g. "US dental clinics", "Shopify brands in the UK"
3. One proof link that opens: Loom, live demo, or public case study (URL, required)
4. Price from, in USD (short text, required) e.g. "$1,500 fixed" or "$60/hour"
5. Overlap you can give, and with which time zone (short text, required) e.g. "4 hours with US Pacific"
6. Name or company, as you want it shown (short text, required)
7. Your LinkedIn URL (URL, required) — note: "Used for our check. Shown publicly only if you tick the next box."
8. Show my LinkedIn link on the card (checkbox)
9. Email (email, required) — note: "Never shown. We send one test message here."
10. I understand pitching and applying are free and Remote Ledger never charges for either (checkbox, required)

## 2 · Request (clients abroad)
Title: "Get this done from India · Remote Ledger"
Intro line: "Free. We publish your request anonymised, collect pitches that pass our five checks, and send you the five best by Friday."
1. Country you're in (short text, required)
2. Industry and company size (short text, required)
3. What you need, in three sentences (long text, required)
4. Budget band (single choice: under $1,000 / $1,000–3,000 / $3,000–10,000 / $10,000+ / monthly retainer)
5. Timeline (short text)
6. Overlap hours you need, and with which time zone (short text)
7. How you'd like the pitches (single choice: one email with five pitches / intro calls)
8. Email (email, required) — note: "Never shown."

## 3 · Waitlist (the full tracker)
Title: "Remote Ledger · full weekly tracker"
Intro line: "Ten roles go on LinkedIn every Monday. The full list, India-eligibility checked, with links, goes to the people on this list."
1. Email (email, required)
2. What you're looking for (multi choice: remote role / contract work / platform work / consulting clients / not sure yet)
3. Would you pay ₹199 a month for the full weekly list? (single choice: yes / maybe / no)
4. One thing you wish a page like this would check for you (long text)
