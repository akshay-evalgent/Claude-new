# Remote Ledger · page setup, with your personal profile kept out of it

Same rules as Founder Signal. Page admins are not shown publicly. Your name appears only if you list the page in your Experience, post about it from your profile, react to its posts from your profile, send follow invites, or get tagged. Do none of those.

1. Desktop browser, logged in to your own account. For Business (grid icon, top right) → Create a Company Page → Company.
2. Name: Remote Ledger. Public URL: remoteledger (fallbacks: theremoteledger, remote-ledger). Website: blank for now. Industry: Staffing and Recruiting. Size: 2–10. Type: Self-employed. Tagline: the line from page-copy.md.
3. Logo: `01-page-setup/logo-400.png`. Cover: `01-page-setup/banner-A-1128x191.png`; if LinkedIn says too small, `banner-A-4200x700.png`. Banner B has the "$85 vs $21" tease on the right; use A for launch, it makes no claim.
4. Tick the verification box, Create page.
5. Edit page → About: paste the About and the Specialties from page-copy.md. Save.
6. Do not add the page to your Experience. Do not invite connections. Do not like or share from your profile. Add one trusted person as Content admin so a second account can post and reply.
7. Make the three Tally forms (10 minutes each, free account at tally.so):
   - **Pitch** (providers in India): Service in 12 words · Who it's for (one kind of client) · One proof link that opens (Loom, live demo, public case study) · Price from, in USD · Hours of overlap you can give, and with which time zone · Company name or your name as you want it shown · LinkedIn URL (for our check; shown only if you tick yes) · Email (not shown) · Checkbox: "I understand pitching is free and Remote Ledger never charges to apply."
   - **Request** (clients abroad): Country · Industry and company size · What you need, in three sentences · Budget band (under $1k / $1–3k / $3–10k / $10k+ / monthly retainer) · Timeline · Overlap hours you need · How you want pitches (email digest of 5 / intro calls) · Email (never shown).
   - **Waitlist** (the paid tracker): Email · What you're looking for (remote role / contract work / platform work / consulting clients) · Would you pay ₹199/month for the full weekly list with India-eligibility checked? (yes / maybe / no).
   Put the Pitch link in the page's Website field and the custom button. All three links go in first comments, never in post text.
8. Make the tracker page. Notion (free) → New page "Remote Ledger tracker" → Import → CSV → `03-tracker/tracker-2026-11-09.csv`. Share → Publish to web. That link is the "full list" in Monday's first comment. Google Sheets works the same way if you prefer.
9. Post Monday's tracker first. Pin the first comment. Reply to every comment within 15 minutes for 90 minutes.
10. Sunday: analytics. Followers, impressions per post, comments per post, Tally submissions this week (pitches, requests, waitlist). Those four numbers decide week two.

## Anonymity, again
Post as the page. Comment as the page. If the post box shows your name at the top, stop and switch. The page replies in the first person plural ("we checked") and never signs a name.
