# Page copy: Remote Ledger

## Name
**Remote Ledger**
Handle to request: `linkedin.com/company/remoteledger`. If taken: `theremoteledger`, then `remote-ledger`.

Why: a ledger is where numbers are written down and checked. The page writes down what remote work for clients abroad really pays and checks that it is open to India. It does not promise dollars from home; it promises the check.

## Tagline (LinkedIn allows 120 characters)
Remote work for clients abroad, checked: open to India, what it really pays, how the money lands.

## About
Three checks on every post.

Open to India? We read the posting's own text, not the job board's "remote" label. Most "remote" roles are US-only. The ones here are not.

What does it really pay? The headline number on a platform's homepage, next to the number on its listings. Both printed, same day, with a screenshot.

How does the money land? Which rail, what it costs on a $2,000 invoice, the FIRA, the purpose code, the LUT, the tax scheme. The paperwork nobody writes down.

Every Monday: roles open to India this week. Tuesday: a pay card. Wednesday: requests from clients abroad. Thursday: vetted pitches from India. Friday: work that isn't a desk job. Saturday: the paperwork. Sunday: a poll.

Pitching is free. Applying is free. We never list anything that charges you to apply.
