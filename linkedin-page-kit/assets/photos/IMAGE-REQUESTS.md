# Image requests, week 1 (2 to 8 November 2026)

How this works: for each post I list the slot, the subject, where to get it, and the credit line to keep. Drop files into `assets/photos/` with the exact filename given, tell me, and I re-render. Sizes: anything over 1200 px on the long side is fine; the generator crops to fit. Stock is fine for scene slots. People and logos: press kits, newsrooms, Wikimedia Commons, or the person's own public post, credited.

Stock searches (any of these work, all free with attribution or CC0): Unsplash `unsplash.com/s/photos/<query>`, Pexels `pexels.com/search/<query>/`, Wikimedia Commons `commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?search=<query>`.
