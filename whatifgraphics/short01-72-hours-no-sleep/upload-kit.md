## 1. Title (max 100 characters, but the Shorts player shows about 40, so the hook must be in the first 40)

**Use this one:**

```
72 Hours Without Sleep: What Happens to YOU, Hour by Hour #Shorts
```

First 40 characters read "72 Hours Without Sleep: What Happens to", which is the whole promise. The number
first, then "YOU" in caps, then the format name so the series is searchable from day one.

A/B alternatives:

1. `Hour 24 is when your brain starts switching off #Shorts`
2. `What 3 days without sleep does to YOU (hour by hour) #Shorts`
3. `Don't stay awake past Hour 36. Here's why. #Shorts`

## 5. Settings and first-hour plan

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Audience | Not made for kids |
| Category | Education (Shorts about the body index better in Education than Entertainment) |
| Related video | Link episode 1 ("What IF You Could Hear What Everyone REALLY Thinks of You?") in the Shorts "Related video" field so the Short feeds the long-form. |
| Remix | Allow (Shorts that get remixed get re-pushed). |
| Pinned comment, within 10 minutes | **"Be honest: what's the longest you've stayed awake? Hour 18, 24, 36, 48 or 72? Reply with the number."** A number question gets more replies than an open one. |
| Reply window | Reply to every comment in the first 2 hours with a one-line fact for their hour (keeps the thread alive and signals engagement). |
| Publish time | Shorts get their first test within minutes, so post when your audience is awake and bored: 20:00 to 22:00 local time of your main market, or 15:00 UTC for a global English audience. |
| Captions | Already burned in. Do not also upload the SRT to a Short; doubled captions look broken. |
