What this means for a 48-hour spike:

- Subscribers are irrelevant. A 1.5K-sub channel with 4 videos got 257K in 4 days. YouTube's browse and

  title, thumbnail and retention gets tested in that stream within hours.

  from shipping five strong premises, not one perfect one.

## 2. The five premise engines that are producing the hits right now

Ranked by evidence (number of channels and size of hits in the last 30 days).

### Engine A: "See everyone's hidden number" (the hottest format by far)
Future Wealth 1.2M, IQ 911K, Crush count 555K, Future Height 465K (all ConChaser, 11-video channel). How Long
Everyone Has Left 1.76M (Ifinite). Love Meter 257K (Nick ifverse, 1.5K subs). Who Has a Crush on You 749K
(Jeremy Bearimy). A visible stat above everyone's head is instantly understood in a thumbnail, and the story
writes itself: look at your crush, your teacher, your parents, a stranger.

Taken: wealth, IQ, crush, height, lifespan, love meter, secrets, fear, regret, mistake, partners, lottery,
betrayal, lies. Still open: who hates you, kill count, bank balance (today, not future), how many people
remember you, stress level, who's lying right now, future job.

### Engine B: "…and Couldn't Stop" (runaway escalation)
You Sped Up 2.2M, Time Sped Up 2.0M, Car Couldn't Stop Speeding Up 432K, Time Ran Backwards 394K (Minute Zero).
Kept Getting Nicer 617K, Kept Getting Richer 441K (All Answers). Every Step Made You Twice as Big 2.4M. The
engine gives a built-in three-act ramp: funny at 2x, useful at 10x, terrifying at 1000x.

Taken: speed, time, size per step, strength, jumping, nicer, richer, evil, IQ up/down, louder, smellier,
attractive. Still open: luckier, faster reactions, more famous, more invisible, younger, more people noticing you.

### Engine C: "Last person on Earth" / crush survival
Can 2 People Restart Humanity 4.7M, Last Guy 556K, Last Girl 513K (Awaken, 8-video channel). Last Man 254K
(Daxter RU). IMAGINE's low-production "You and Your Crush Were the Last People" still did 52K in 2 days.
Romance plus survival plus "what would you actually do" is the most commented-on engine.

### Engine D: Numbers-first superpowers (reaction time, IQ)
Reaction Time 10x Faster 4.7M, 500 IQ 1.4M, Used 500 IQ to Disappear 548K (Ifverse). Infinite IQ at School
283K (IFopposite, 5K subs). Infinite IQ 139K. Teacher Had Infinite IQ 84K in 1 day. The number in the title does
the work. "School" as the setting consistently lifts the IQ premise.

### Engine E: Regression / "all your memories"
Woke Up in Kindergarten With All Your Memories 415K (Ifverse), Went Back 10 Years With All Your Memories 51K in
20 hours (ConChaser), Woke Up as a Kid Again 24K. This is the manhwa "regressor" trope and the audience that
reads those is exactly the 13 to 24 male audience watching this niche. Only lightly mined so far.

### Engine F: Game logic in real life
Whole World Became Roblox 456K (Skylox). Creative Mode 524K, Respawn 319K, Level Up 192K, Real-Life Tips Like
Video Games 106K (Jeremy Bearimy). Infinite Robux 5K (bad execution, not bad premise). Roblox and Minecraft
vocabulary in the title pulls the under-16 audience in from gaming feeds.

## 4. The 5 best ideas for whatifgraphics

Each one combines a proven engine with a premise nobody in the niche has shipped yet. All are buildable with the
episode-1 pipeline (same mascot, same side cast, a handful of new props).

### Idea 1 — What IF You Could See Who Secretly HATES You?
- **Engine:** A (hidden number). The inverse of the two biggest crush videos in the niche (749K and 555K). Negative
  reveals out-click positive ones, and nobody has made this one.
- **Hook (first 20 s):** You wake up and a red skull floats over your best friend's head. Then your mum's. Then,
  worst of all, nothing over your crush. She doesn't hate you. She doesn't think about you at all.
- **Beats:** test it at school (the quiet kid hates you, the bully doesn't), use it to win (avoid backstabbers,
  pick a group project team), escalate (go public, politicians, your own reflection has a skull), cost (you learn
  WHY people hate you and most of it is fair), choice (fix yourself or stop looking).
- **Thumbnail:** mascot in a crowd, every head has a tiny red skull, one person with a heart. Text: **WHO HATES YOU**.
- **Why 48 h:** rides the exact "see everyone's X" wave ConChaser is being fed on right now, and the thumbnail
  reads from the feed in half a second.

### Idea 2 — What IF Your Reaction Time Kept Getting FASTER and Couldn't Stop?
- **Engine:** B (couldn't stop) × D (numbers-first). The single biggest video in the niche this month was
  "Reaction Time 10x Faster" (4.7M). The "couldn't stop" channels have never used reaction time.
- **Hook:** Day 1 you catch a falling glass. Day 7 you can't lose at any game. Day 30 you dodge a car. Day 60,
  a conversation takes an hour because everyone talks so slowly. Day 90, a blink lasts a year.
- **Beats:** small wins (sport, games, esports fame), escalation (fastest human alive, the lab), cost (the world
  freezes, loneliness, you can't hear music any more), the choice (a scientist offers to slow you back down,
  but you'd lose the one moment you can relive forever).
- **Thumbnail:** mascot mid-dodge, five objects frozen around him, speed lines. Text: **DAY 1 → DAY 100** with
  a "10x… 100x… ∞" counter.
- **Why 48 h:** two proven titles fused; the escalation structure keeps retention high through minute 8.

### Idea 3 — What IF You and Your Crush Were the Last Two People on Earth?
- **Engine:** C (last on Earth). Awaken's 4.7M, 556K and 513K all come from this engine, and the only crush
  version so far is a 52K low-effort one. Romance plus survival is the most commented premise in the niche.
- **Hook:** Everyone vanishes mid-sentence. You walk the empty city for three days. On day four you find a note
  on the school gate in handwriting you recognise.
- **Beats:** the awkward first week (she doesn't like you, that's the whole point), survival montage, the one
  rule you agree on, a twist (a third footprint), the real question (is it love or just the only option), the
  ending (people come back, and she has to decide if she still talks to you).
- **Thumbnail:** two characters on an empty road between dark skyscrapers, one shared thought bubble with a
  question mark. Text: **LAST 2 ON EARTH**.
- **Why 48 h:** highest comment and share potential of the five; "what would you do" comments drive the early
  engagement signal.

### Idea 4 — What IF You Went Back to Day 1 of School With All Your Memories?
- **Engine:** E (regression). Kindergarten version did 415K; "back 10 years" got 51K in its first 20 hours.
  The manhwa "regressor" audience is huge and barely served by animated what-if.
- **Hook:** You wake up, you're 11, and you know every exam answer, every fight, every crush, and exactly which
  day your dad loses his job.
- **Beats:** cheating gloriously (tests, sports, predicting the fire alarm), fixing things (saving a friendship
  you lost), escalation (the stock tip, the fame), cost (your memories make you a stranger to your own friends,
  the one thing you can't change), choice (live the loop again, or let the future be unknown).
- **Thumbnail:** mascot small at a tiny desk, giant thought bubble full of future images (phone, car, a wedding),
  classmates confused. Text: **DAY 1 AGAIN**.
- **Why 48 h:** nostalgia plus power fantasy, strong with the 18 to 30 segment that comments most.

### Idea 5 — What IF You Had ADMIN Commands in Real Life?
- **Engine:** F (game logic). Creative Mode did 524K, Whole World Became Roblox 456K, Respawn 319K. "Admin
  commands" is one of the most searched Roblox phrases and no one has used it as a what-if.
- **Hook:** A console appears in front of your eyes. You type `/kick` on the kid who took your seat. He vanishes.
  He's fine. He's in the car park. You have a new problem.
- **Beats:** `/fly`, `/tp school`, `/give money`, `/speed 10`, the rules (commands cost "trust", nobody can see the
  console, every command gets logged), escalation (`/ban` a bully, `/weather snow` on a Tuesday, fame), cost
  (`/undo` doesn't exist, someone finds the log), the choice (`/op` someone else, or `/deop` yourself).
- **Thumbnail:** mascot with a glowing command line floating in front of him, a classmate mid-teleport. Text:
  **/admin** in a Minecraft-style pixel font.
- **Why 48 h:** gaming vocabulary pulls from Roblox and Minecraft feeds, which are the largest adjacent
  audiences on the platform; Skylox's Roblox crossover shows the handoff works.

### Production order
Ship Idea 1 first (fastest to make, hottest engine), then 3, then 2, 4, 5. Three days apart, never two in 48
hours (the second cannibalises the first in the test audience).

## 5. The 48-hour launch checklist (what the hit channels actually do)

1. **Title formula, no exceptions:** `What IF You [verb] [concrete premise]?`, one word in CAPS, under 60 chars.
2. **Thumbnail test:** one mascot, one visible "mechanic" (bubble, stat, console, skull), 1 to 3 words. If CTR
   is under 4% at 24 h, swap thumbnail text, not the image.
3. **Runtime 9 to 12 minutes,** retention target 45% average view duration. The cost act (minute 6 to 8) is where
   competitors lose viewers; keep the stakes rising, not falling.
4. **First 30 seconds:** the power is already active. No "today we're going to explore". Competitors that open
   with setup (Kian Odyssey, median 3.8K) die; ones that open mid-scene (ConChaser, median 191K) live.
5. **Publish 16:00 to 18:00 UK time.** Pin a one-emoji poll comment in the first ten minutes, reply to every
   comment for two hours.
6. **Three Shorts** from each episode, posted at +6 h, +24 h, +48 h, each ending on a cliffhanger line and
   pointing to the long video. Treat them as feeders, not as the product.
7. **Series hooks:** Ifinite and Ifverse now run "Part 4", "Part 5" and "FULL MOVIE" versions of their hits.
   Any idea above that clears 300K gets a Part 2 within 7 days.
8. **Behind-the-scenes Short:** "I made this whole episode with Claude" is a trending search right now
   (Mr omi, 364K in two days). One Short showing the pipeline costs nothing and pulls a second audience.
