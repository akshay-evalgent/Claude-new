# EP01: 5 Pets Compete to Get Adopted

**Format:** Would you…? with a running score (style bible §3)
**Premise (one sentence):** A kid can adopt only one pet, so five pets compete in a game show to win her over. The one who wins is the one who answers honestly.
**Title:** 5 Pets Compete to Get Adopted (29 characters, plus the signature emoji once it's chosen)
**Accent colour:** `#ff5a7a` (adoption pink)
**Sets:** plains game-show stage, a "lava" pit and a fetch field. All three are dressings of the library's **Plains field**, so no new set build is needed.
**Runtime:** 3:08 · 66 shots · average shot 2.85 s (re-timed to the recorded voice-over)
**Dialogue word count:** 199, below the bible's 330–400 target. This is on purpose: about half of the beats are physical gags (bowl swipe, faceplant, fake lava, scoreboard explosion, fence crash). If the VO cast want more, the setup and Round 1 have room for extra lines.

**Change from the pitch:** in the pitch, Ava adopted the stray. Here she picks **Lou**, which pays off the midpoint, then takes everyone, and the stray turns out to be Greg. That keeps the twist and gives the ending real emotional weight.

## Script

Key words that the captions highlight in the accent colour are marked *like this*.

### COLD OPEN: STAGE, DAY
**AVA** (holding up a sign: ADOPT 1): I can only adopt *ONE* of you.
*Smash punch-in on the row. Rex, Duchess and Pico are shocked; Greg is blank. Lou is asleep in her bucket.*
**REX:** …*ONE?!*

### SETUP
**AVA:** Welcome to *Would You Rather:* Pets Edition!
**REX** (tail wagging): Rex! I love you *already!*
**DUCHESS** (smug, crown): Duchess. I *accept* your application.
**PICO:** Pico! Pico! *PICO!*
*Lou snores.*
**AVA** (O.S.): And… *Lou?*
*Greg is chewing his podium. He looks up.*
**GREG:** Baaa.

### ROUND 1: "WOULD YOU SHARE YOUR FOOD?"
**AVA:** Round one! Would you *share* your food?
**REX** (shoves his bowl forward): YES! Take *ALL* of it! *(stares at the bowl; tears well up)*
**DUCHESS:** Define… *share.* *(swipes her bowl off the podium; crash)*
**PICO:** YES! Take *ALL* of it!
*Rex side-eyes Pico. Lou: still asleep.*
**AVA:** Greg, would you share your—
*Greg eats the question card out of her hand. Ava turns to camera.*
**AVA:** …that was my *card.*
**AVA:** Rex, one point. Pico… also one, I guess.
*(Greg: −1, buzzer.)*
**AVA** (to the audience, back to the stage): Okay! Round two, coming up!
*Behind her, Duchess sneaks to the scoreboard and changes her 0 to a 9. Pico sees.*
**DUCHESS:** Hm.

### ROUND 2: THE "LAVA" PIT
*Ava stands on a pillar over a glowing orange pit. Sign: LAVA (REAL).*
**AVA:** Round two! I'm in *danger!* There's a *cookie.* Who do you save?
**REX:** I'M COMING, *AVA!* *(trips over Lou's bucket and faceplants)* …I'm *okay!*
*Duchess strolls to the cookie pillar and eats it.*
**DUCHESS:** Delicious *choice.*
**PICO** (flying in): SAVE THE *COOKIE!* *(finds crumbs)*
**GREG:** BAAA! *(charges straight across the "lava" and rams the pillar)*
**AVA:** Wait— *WAIT!* *(falls in; soft poof)*
**AVA** (lying in it): It's… *wool.* It was always wool.
**AVA:** Duchess, why do you have *nine?*
**DUCHESS:** Pure *talent.*
**AVA** (O.S.): Rex at least *tried.*
**REX** (face in the dirt, tail wagging): I *tried!*

### MIDPOINT: ROUND 3, 3 AM
*Time-lapse to night.*
**AVA** (whispering): Round three. It's *3 AM.* Can you stay quiet?
*Crickets. Everyone holds still… side-eyes…*
**REX:** AWOOOOO! *(howls at the moon)*
*Duchess knocks the flower pot off her podium, then looks at camera.*
**PICO:** THREE A.M.! *THREE A.M.!*
*Greg charges the scoreboard. It explodes into blocks. The scores become ???.*
**AVA** (in the dust): …okay. New plan. One question. Be *honest.*
*Sunrise.*
**AVA:** Why do you want a *home?*
**REX:** *SNACKS!*
**DUCHESS:** *Servants.*
**PICO:** SNACKS! *SERVANTS!*
**GREG:** Baaa.
*The music drops out. Lou wakes and slowly rises out of the bucket.*
**LOU:** …so I'm not… *alone.*
*Everyone turns. Rex tears up.*
**PICO** (softly, and meaning it): …not *alone.*

### FINAL ROUND: FETCH
**AVA** (wiping her eye): Okay. Final round… Go *FETCH!* *(throws a stick far across the field)*
*Rex launches. Greg charges; Duchess jumps on his back. Pico grabs Lou's bucket and flies.*
**DUCHESS** (riding): Faster, *goat!*
*Rex clears the fence. Greg smashes through it; Duchess goes flying.*
**AVA** (O.S.): GREG! You're *DISQUALIFIED!*
*Rex gets to the stick first. Victory hop. Pico arrives, exhausted, and sets Lou down. Rex walks over and drops the stick in Lou's bucket.*
**REX:** You should have it.
**LOU:** …thanks, *Rex.*
**PICO:** Thanks, *Rex!*
**DUCHESS** (dusting herself off): Ugh. Fine. That was *sweet.*

### PAYOFF: STAGE
*Greg's podium is empty.*
**AVA:** I've *decided.* *(drumroll)* I choose…
*Quick hopeful close-ups: Rex, Duchess (pretending not to care), Pico, Lou.*
**AVA** (lifting Lou's bucket): *Lou.*
*Hearts. Rex, crushed, whimpers.*
**AVA:** …and *Rex.* And *Pico.* And… fine, *Duchess.*
*Everyone cheers.*

### BUTTON
*A whimper. A scruffy stray dog steps out from behind a tree. Name tag: ???. Small horns poke out of its fur.*
**AVA** (O.S., melting): Aww… you too, *buddy?*
*She kneels. The stray headbutts her. The disguise falls off: it's Greg.*
**GREG:** Baaa.
**DUCHESS:** …*obviously.*

### SEQUEL HOOK
*Ava walks home carrying Lou, the pets in a line behind her. In the foreground, the sign: ADOPT ~~1~~ 5.*
**MOM** (O.S.): AVA! I SAID *ONE!*
*Everyone freezes and turns to camera. Blank faces.*
*End card: NEXT EPISODE: THE PETS MEET MOM. Greg wanders back and eats the sign. Fade out.*
