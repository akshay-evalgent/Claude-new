This girl fell three kilometers out of the sky, still strapped to her seat. And she survived.
On Christmas Eve, 1971, seventeen-year-old Juliane Koepcke was flying over the Amazon with her mother.
Then the plane flew into a thunderstorm. Lightning struck, and it broke apart in mid-air.
She woke up on the jungle floor, alone. Her collarbone was broken, and her mother was gone.
All she had was a small bag of candy.
But she remembered her father's advice. Find water, and follow it.
So she followed a stream, then a river. For days.
Maggots got into a wound on her arm.
On the tenth day, she found a boat, and fuel. She poured it on the wound, to drive the maggots out.
Then, lumberjacks found her. They thought she was a water spirit.
She was the only survivor, out of ninety-two people.
Today, she's a biologist, and runs a research station in that same rainforest.
